title: Chronology at points comes in three strictly different strengths

`ChronalNeighbours` (every point, both sides, chronology in every open) ⇒ `NonemptyChronalCones`
(I⁺(x), I⁻(x) non-empty in M; the inline `hI` of `Zplus_Zminus_eq_univ`, `K_lorentzien`,
`K_eq_I_of_pushUp`) ⇒ `HasChronalNeighbourEverywhere` (one side, in M), strictly
(`Causal.ChronalNeighbourBridge`). The first two agree under causal arc-connectedness (no
separation axiom) or under absolute minimality on a T1 space; neither hypothesis can be dropped
(`globalLine`: ≪ only on ℝ itself; `sierpinskiCausality` on `Prop`, absolutely minimal, not T1 — note
that `Prop` IS T0 once `Topology.IsScott` is imported). The converse fails on `closedHalfLine`
(T-abs-chronal-neighbour-bridge).
