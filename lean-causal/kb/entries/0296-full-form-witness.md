To write one witness that decides every causality-sort node for a model (`Causal.isolatedLine_separates`, Causal/IsolatedLineConjoined.lean), take the has/lacks lists of `ladder.counterexample` for it, state the library's facts as conjuncts, and let `causal_ladder` close the ids the ladder's implications give (push-up, its local forms, the causally open forms, causality, curves extend). The isolated line turned out globally hyperbolic and causally compact with a time function (every diamond is `[p, q]`, `∅` or `{0}`) and still not tarentin, an either-reading witness of GH ∧ plain ∧ causally open ⇏ tarentin. A leaf module may import `Causal.Ladder.Rules` for `causal_ladder` (kb/0221).
