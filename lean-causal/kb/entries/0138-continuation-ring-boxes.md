Use the disc charts centred on a circle of radius `ρ`, `ρ` small, chained at angle steps `sin(w)/2`: `Causal.EAlphaContinuation.exists_smul_devPt_of_ringBox`. Its box form asks only that the map be a translate of the sector chart on each box (`ringBox`), with no continuity and no point of the line. Caution for (b′) at `E_κ`: the box form needs one map into `E^{1,2}` on all the boxes, which a chart into `E_κ′` does not give directly (T-ett-ealpha-gx-structure).
