Found by T-ett-ealpha-sectors. In the cone C_alpha, the ball B(p, r_p sin w) around a regular point lies in the flat sector of half-opening w (angle_lt_of_coneE_lt), so balls spare the proof that sectors are open. The polar map of a sector need not preserve the cone distance beyond opening alpha/2, even below min(pi, alpha): fromPlane_wide_not_isometric at alpha = pi/2 (a sector of opening 9pi/20 holding the directions 0 and 2pi/5). It is no general bound (at alpha = 2pi, C_2pi is the plane); the discs avoid the question.
