title: A cheap plain, causally open, arc-connected line with a chronologically isolated point
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-20
---
`Causal.isolatedLine`: the timelike line on ℝ ∖ {0}, with 0 related only to itself. It is causally
arc-connected, causally open and causally plain, and refutes any claim that these give chronology
at every point, or a chronal curve leaving a proper connected open set ((0, ∞))
(`Causal.LocateExtension`, T-abs-locate-extension).
