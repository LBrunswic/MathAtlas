`Causal.DiamondConfinement`: a chronal homotopy with fixed endpoints whose first curve lies in `Ũ` stays in `Ũ` once the closure of the `Ũ`-diamond lies in `Ũ` (`ChronalHomotopyOn.mapsTo_of_closure_subset`, any causality, no separation axiom: the set of good parameters is open by the tube lemma and closed by the closure hypothesis). With `W` causally arc-connected and future one-connected, the `W`-diamond is the `W ⊓ Ũ`-diamond as soon as one chronal curve of `W` from `x` to `y` lies in `Ũ` (`Iplus_inter_Iminus_eq_inf_…`). The localisation `LocallyStronglyCausal` (two-sided; `StronglyCausal.locallyStronglyCausal`) gives the closure hypothesis near every point on a locally compact `R₁` space (`LocallyStronglyCausal.exists_isCompact_closure`); what (i)–(ii) use is the weaker closure property itself, which Sbierski gets from his cone estimates (3.2). Sbierski's Step 3 also needs `γ̃(−s₁) ≪_Ũ γ̃(0)` (Step 1.1, :491), left implicit (T-abs-diamond-confinement).
