Absolute minimality without arc-connectedness, for a polygonal causality. If every kind of step subdivides at rational parameters into steps of the same kind, Causal.transGen_of_segment_subdivision gives AbsolutelyMinimalOn W for every W and every cover: the equal subdivision at k/n, below a Lebesgue number of the compact segment. Causal and timelike segments subdivide at any parameter, a rational jump only at rational ones. Both halves of AbsolutelyMinimalOn (≤ and ≪) go this way. Causal.rationalJumpPlane is the either-reading witness of chronal_neighbours ⇏ causally_arc_connected: no causal curve joins (0, 0) ≤ (0, 1), by the rational line's intermediate-value argument (T-abs-chronal-not-arc-connected).
