Minkowski's ≪ plus extra spacelike relations ("spokes") inside an achronal slice
(`spokesCausality`, `Causal.KBorelien`): `≤_U = base ∨ base∘spoke∘base` is transitive because the
base relates two points of one slice only when they are equal; absolute minimality follows from
`exists_subdivision_Icc` along each spoke. Put an uncountable family of extra relations in an
achronal set: elsewhere the openness of ≪ absorbs all but countably many. A local witness, unlike
`ofRelation` ones (kb/0014, kb/0019, kb/0030) (T-ett-k-borelien).
