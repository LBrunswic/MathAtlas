title: harc, the order realised by curves: what the lifting of strong causality uses

The lifting of strong causality (`Causal.StrongCausalityLifts`) uses of `M̃` only `harc : ∀ a z, C.le ⊤ a z → ∃ c, C.CausalCurveOn ⊤ c 0 1 ∧ c 0 = a ∧ c 1 = z`, weaker than `CausallyArcConnected` (`orderLine` has harc and is not causally arc-connected, no-gluing reading): the `_of_le_realised` forms (`Causal.StrongCausalityLiftsCurves`) are the statement OQ-145 offers. Without harc the printed form fails even with gluing and continuous maps (`not_stronglyCausal_lift_of_curves_glued`, on `symmRationalLine`). The instantiating targets (BTZ, Ẽ_α) need harc for their causalities, which their curve-defined causalities give.
