title: A curve with no limit at its end, or leaving an open set, is future-inextensible
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-23
---
`Causality.futureInextensible_of_forall_not_tendsto` (`Causal.SchwCausalLemmas`): a causal curve of `W` over `I` with `IsLUB I b`, `b ∉ I` and no limit in `W` at `b⁻` is future-inextensible in `W`, with no separation axiom and no time function. With T2, a curve of `M` over `[a, b]` lying in `W` on `[a, b)` with `γ b ∉ W` is (`futureInextensible_of_leaving`); T2 is needed there (`forkPoint_leaving_futureExtensible`).
