`Causal.SingularRowsCauchyExt.cauchyExtension`: for any Cauchy time function T of E₀ strictly increasing along ≤ (`exists_cauchyTimeFunction`), {T < a} with its natural causality is a globally hyperbolic, connected E₀-manifold (a SingularFlatSpacetime) and its inclusion an IsCauchyEmbedding, not onto. The frontier T⁻¹(a) has BTZ points (τ-orbit of the axis) and regular ones (τ-orbit of (0,1)), found with `exists_tauFlow_level`. Being a past set, it makes the rows' conclusions immediate: it witnesses hypotheses, not proofs (T-ett-cauchy-ext-e0).
