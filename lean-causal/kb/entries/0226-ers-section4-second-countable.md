`…_secondCountable` (Causal.ErsSecondCountable, Causal.Ers464SecondCountable) is the original less discharged binders: `hext`, the through-hypotheses, and `hsplit`/`hacausal` only where the original also has `S` Cauchy in the same `W` for the same causality (plus `W` causally arc-connected for `hacausal`, kb/0218). `…_of_isCauchySurfaceOn_secondCountable` trades `hacausal`, `hsplit` for `IsCauchySurfaceOn U₁ S` and `CausallyArcConnectedOn U₁` (DEC-162): a sibling, not a strengthening. Together no ERS §4 theorem needs curves_extend, InextensibleThrough, SplitBy or IsAcausalOn except Thm 4.75's IsLocalDevelopment, maxDevelopmentIn_eq, ers_theorem_4_64_in and ers_prop_4_63 (OQ-41's reading).
