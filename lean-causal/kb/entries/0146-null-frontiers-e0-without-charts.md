E₀'s vertical lines `τ ↦ (τ, z)` are null, not chronological, so ConeNull's vertical sections do not apply. Use the chronological rays `r ↦ (u + r, r e^{iθ})` (`Causal.E0.lt_ray`): the frontier of any `≪`-past or `≪`-future set, open or not, meets each ray at most once. Then the λ₃-preserving shear `(τ, z) ↦ (τ − |z|, z)` and polar coordinates: `Causal.MeasuresE0.volume_eq_zero_of_shear_ray_subsingleton`, `Causal.E0.volume_frontier_of_past` / `_of_future` (T-ett-measures-e0).
