title: State new theorems with the named ladder side conditions
kind: house-rule
created: 2026-11-20
---
The ladder's localisation side conditions have names in `Causal.LadderLocal`: `LocallyPushUp`,
`LocallyCausallyOpenFuture`, `LocallyCausallyOpen`, `ChronalNeighbours` (past form
`ChronalPastNeighbours`), `NoCausalEndpoint`, `OneSidedBoundary`. State a new theorem with them,
not with the inline ∀-forms, so that once the keeper makes them ladder nodes it can be a forward
rule of `causal_ladder` (T-abs-ladder-local-defs).
