title: Refuting gluing: a relation never crosses between opens with disjoint closures
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-22
---
`Precausality.AbsolutelyMinimalOn.mem_of_le_sup` (`Causal.GluingGeneric.Basic`) and `Causal.GluingModels.le_mem_of_split`: an absolutely minimal relation never crosses between open sets whose closures do not meet (the cover `{M ∖ cl B, M ∖ cl A}`). Hence `Causality.ofRelation` glues iff its relations lie in the diagonal (T2 for the hereditary form, T₂.₅ for the M-only one): every `ofRelation` witness off the diagonal (totalLine, fan, bowtie, puncturedPlane, …) is no causality under gluing. On a discrete space an absolutely minimal ≤ is the diagonal.
