`Causal.halfLightLine` (ℝ, ≪ empty, x ≤_U y iff x = y ∈ U or 0 ≤ x ≤ y with [x, y] ⊆ U) is
absolutely minimal on every open set for every cover (the gluing reading of `defi:causalite`, OQ-18),
causally arc-connected, GH, strongly causal, with a time function, causally open in the future and
not in the past (J⁻_M((1,2)) = [0,2)); its failure of local causal openness sits exactly at 0
(`Causal.Dec8MinimalWitness`). So the printed J⁺-only causal openness is strictly weaker than DEC-8's
two-sided one in both readings. Its ≪ is empty and it is not tarentin: it says nothing about
chronological or tarentin spacetimes (T-abs-dec8-minimal-witness).
