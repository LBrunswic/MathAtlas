title: Push-up on a polygonal causality from the openness of I±_U alone
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-21
---
Push-up on a polygonal causality from the openness of I±_U alone: for x ≪ y ≤ z, induct on the causal chain; I⁺_U(x) is open, so it contains y − (ε, ε), from which a causal step y → z is chronal. For x ≤ y ≪ z, head-induct; I⁻_U(z) is open and contains y + (ε, ε) when there is room (here from y's first chronal step), at which the step x → y is chronal. No tilted chain with decreasing εᵢ is needed (Causal.notchedCausality_pushUp).
