Static spacetimes: a chart of space that preserves distances on its source, times the identity of time, is a ChartIso of the static causalities (staticCausality.chartIso_prod_isometricOn), because staticLe/staticLt read distances along curves only. Build such a chart with EAlphaSector.ofIsometricOn: it takes an isometric map from an open set onto an open set, uses invFunOn as the inverse, and gets continuity from the isometry.
