id: 0030
title: A causal relation need not be closed, even locally: test against chronoMinkowski
kind: pitfall
created: 2026-11-20
---
The reflexive chronological causality `C.reflChrono` (x ≤ y iff x = y or x ≪ y;
`CausalScaffold.GerochCauchy`) keeps tarentin, causal arc-connectedness, strong causality (both
readings), plainness, causal openness, push-up, absolute minimality and admissible measures, yet
breaks every compactness of diamonds. On Minkowski space it is `Causal.chronoMinkowski n`, and it
refutes ETT's printed Geroch (iii)⇒(i) (OQ-32). Test any compactness or limit-curve claim against
`chronoMinkowski n` before trying to prove it (T-ett-geroch-iii-i).
