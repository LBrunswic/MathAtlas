title: CommonSubExtension.Equivalent is directional: fix the reading before refuting uniqueness
kind: pitfall
created: 2026-11-20
---
`CommonSubExtension.Equivalent` (and `CommonSubDev.Equivalent`) is a bijective morphism X → Y;
abstractly its inverse need not be a morphism (`DensWitness.base_equivalent_bigSub`), and the
one-step symmetric relation is not transitive. "No morphism either way"
(`cauchyPgcdAbstract_counterexample_symm`) refutes every reading asking for a morphism between the
two, not the equivalence generated by bijective morphisms (which holds through the base when i₁ is
bijective). Over a Geometry all readings coincide (`isMor_inv`) (T-ett-subextension).
