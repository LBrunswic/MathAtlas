title: Show a dual rule is really used: erase it and watch examples fail
kind: pitfall
created: 2026-11-23
---
To check a new `causal_ladder` rule pair is what closes its examples, erase the pair and confirm the examples fail (T-tac-dual-has-chronal-neighbour: exactly 4 DualTest examples). Beware positives that close without it: `C.dual.dual` is `C` by `rfl`, so aesop's builtin `assumption` can close a goal about `C.dual.dual` from a hypothesis about `C`; write the positives with an odd number of reversals. Causal/Ladder/DualTest.lean is generated by `campaign/T-tac-ladder-dual/dual_closure.py`, which reads its edges off the shipped Rules.lean: rerun it after every emit-lean.
