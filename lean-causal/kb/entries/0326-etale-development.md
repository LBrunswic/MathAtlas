`Gluing.CompatibleAlong` of the subtype pieces of an Einstein-style gluing is `AgreeOn C₁ C₂ A` (`EtaleGluing.compatibleAlong_iff_agreeOn`); `CausalityFollows` has a `≪` twin, `CausalityFollowsLt` (`Causal.ErsEtaleMghd`). The gluing of two developments along a common development closed in `U₁ ∩ U₂` is a Hausdorff, causal `TwoPiece` spacetime étale over `M` with `S` lifted Cauchy (`EtaleGluing.isEtaleDevelopment`, `ers_etale_development`); the pieces need antisymmetry and causal arc-connectedness only, absolute minimality follows.
