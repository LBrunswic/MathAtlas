Sbierski 2312.07443's (2.4) is `Causal.Causality.PastInextensible.exists_mem_of_mem_Jplus` (`Causal.PastInextensibleMeetsCauchy`): on T1, second countable spaces, with `S` Cauchy in `W`, `W` causally arc-connected and `≤_W` antisymmetric, every past-inextensible causal curve of `W` with a point in `J⁺(S, W)` meets `S`; no tail-freeness is asked. The time reversal is `FutureInextensible.exists_mem_of_mem_Jminus`, Sbierski's `I⁺` form `exists_mem_of_mem_Iplus`, and the ladder form at `M` `…_of_isCausal`. The extension that keeps the reparametrisation is the strict library's `exists_subArc_of_tailFree` (kb/0243).
