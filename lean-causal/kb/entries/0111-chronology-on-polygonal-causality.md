A chronology on a polygonal causality. Take ≪_U := TransGen of a strict step whose side condition is closed in the start and open in the end (here a ∈ R̄ → b ∈ R). Then isOpen_lt_right and isOpen_lt_left reduce to moving the end of the last step or the start of the first (chronalMembraneStep.eventually_right, .eventually_left). The segment stays in U by Causal.eventually_segment_subset_of_isOpen (a thickening of the compact segment). The naive condition a ∈ R → b ∈ R stays open when the end moves (R is open) but not when the start moves, and gives no causality (Causal.not_exists_causality_naive_membrane_chronology).
