Causal openness of a polygonal causality without push-up. If the kinds of step are invariant under a time translation, move the chain by −(s, 0): Causal.rationalJumpStep.eventually_shift keeps the moved segment in U by a thickening of the compact segment. Then add one timelike step to y; J⁺ is open, and J⁻ likewise. Causal.rationalJumpPlane is causally open in the future and in the past, with chronal neighbours, absolutely minimal on every open set, and push-up fails in every non-empty open set. So push_up ∧ chronal_neighbours ⇒ causally_open (Lemma K's route) does not reverse, not even under absolute minimality (Causal.not_locallyPushUp_of_chronalNeighbours_causallyOpen_bare) (T-abs-chronal-not-arc-connected).
