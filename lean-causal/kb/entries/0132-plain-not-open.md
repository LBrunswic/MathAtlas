The notched plane: an either-reading witness that causal plainness gives no causal openness. Absolutely minimal on every open set, plain, push-up, GH, strongly causal, a time function, causally open in the future; not causally open in the past, nor locally at the corner, no chronal neighbours, not tarentin; its time reversal fails future openness, global and local (Causal.notchedCausality_separates, notchedCausality_dual_separates). The ladder forces such a model to be push-up (absolutely_minimal ∧ causally_plain ⇒ push_up, T1) and without chronal neighbours (push_up ∧ chronal_neighbours ⇒ causally_open): both hold (T-abs-plain-not-open).
