`Causal.Causality.StronglyCausal.stronglyCausalOpen_of_causallyOpen` (`Causal.SCOpenWithoutTarentin`): for `O` the interior of a causally convex neighbourhood, the hull `J⁺_M(O) ∩ J⁻_M(O)` is the open convex neighbourhood; weaker forms `_of_isOpen_Jplus_Jminus` (only `J^±_M` of open sets open) and `_of_isOpen_hull`. A set is causally convex in `M` iff it contains its hull (`causallyConvexIn_top_iff`). kb/0117 sharpened: E₀'s `StronglyCausalOpen` follows from its strong causality and `E0.isOpen_Jplus`/`isOpen_Jminus` through `_of_isOpen_Jplus_Jminus`. The future half alone does not open the hull (the one-way membrane, `oneWayMembrane_hull_not_isOpen`), though the membrane is `StronglyCausalOpen`: whether `causally_open_future` suffices is open.
