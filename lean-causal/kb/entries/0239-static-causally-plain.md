`Causal.staticCausality.causallyPlain` (`Causal.Model.EAlphaPlain`): every static spacetime ℝ × X over a geodesic metric space (no completeness, no properness) is causally plain, by `PushUp.causallyPlain_of_chronalNeighbours` from `staticCausality.pushUp` and `.chronalNeighbours`; one-line instances `cosmicString_causallyPlain` (the 3+1 form of record, DEC-188), `massive_causallyPlain` (every α > 0), their regular parts, `minkowski_causallyPlain`, `halfPlane_causallyPlain` (DEC-168, T-loc-ealpha-plain). E≥0-manifolds: `Causal.SFALocal.causallyPlain`.
