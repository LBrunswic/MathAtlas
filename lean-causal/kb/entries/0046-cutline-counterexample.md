title: A cheap local counterexample for nesting questions: the cut line
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-20
---
`Causal.cutLine a`, the timelike line in which `a` has no causal past, is local, causal and nested
in `timelikeLine`. It refutes "wider GH ⇒ narrower GH" (`a = 0`: the diamond from −1 to 1 is
[−1, 1] ∖ {0}) and "wider Cauchy ⇒ narrower Cauchy" (`a = 1`, S = {0})
(`Causal.NestedCausalities`, T-abs-nested-causalities).
