title: The Lemma K family is ETT-layer; its model corollaries are in Causal.Model.LemmaKModels
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-23
---
Since T-abs-hoist-lemma-k, `Causal.LemmaKBare`, `LemmaKReduction`, `LemmaKSC`, `LemmaKLocal`, `LevinUtility`, `LemmaKSCLocal`, `LemmaK` and `LemmaKObstruction` import no model, so a layer-1 module may import Lemma K, including `Causality.causallyOpen_of_pushUp`. The static, E_α and Minkowski corollaries are in the model module `Causal.Model.LemmaKModels` (same full names), and `Causality.CurvesExtend.dual` is in `Causal.CauchyExtension`.
