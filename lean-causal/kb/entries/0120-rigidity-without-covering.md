`Causal.BPrimeE0` proves BTZ_1's `prop:isom` at α = 0 without lifting to a cover. (1) On a slit box the reference sector chart is a single chart, and the class of the reading isometry modulo the holonomy is locally constant (`HolRel`, `IsPreconnected.induction₂'`), so one isometry `g` develops the map on the whole box (`exists_isom_dev`). (2) Limits along a ray put `g`'s image of the developed axis on the axis line (`smul_null_line`). (3) The stabiliser of that line (`isom_of_null_line`), then injectivity or continuity across the slit (`mu_eq_one`). The scheme may serve `E_κ`, κ > 0, whose axis is timelike (T-ett-bprime-ealpha) (T-ett-bprime-e0).
