---
id: 0022
title: After a ruling, the strengthened theorem is principal and the printed form becomes a Prop
kind: house-rule
created: 2026-10-30
---
When the author rules that a statement carries a hypothesis it does not print (DEC-15, DEC-18,
DEC-19), the theorem with that hypothesis becomes the declaration the label names, the
hypothesis row reads "carried", and the printed form — if believed false — is kept as a `Prop`
definition with its finding, never as a theorem resting on `sorry`. A differentiable object a
proof uses (the flow of `lem:vecteur`) can be a data-carrying named hypothesis with exactly the
C⁰ properties the proof asserts (`VecteurFlow`); say which of them the paper asserts rather than
argues.
