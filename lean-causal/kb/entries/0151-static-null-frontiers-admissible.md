title: Null frontiers and admissible measures on any static ℝ × X
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-21
---
For `ν` any s-finite measure on `X`, the frontier of any `≪`-past or `≪`-future set, open or not, is `dt ⊗ ν`-null (`Causal.MeasuresEAlpha.static_prod_frontier_of_past`, `_of_future`: ConeNull's vertical sections, no Lebesgue measure on `X`). With `ν` positive on open sets and `X` σ-compact, every finite measure with the null sets of `dt ⊗ ν` is admissible in the past and in the future (`static_admissible_of_equiv`) and restricts to every causally convex open set (`static_admissiblePastOn_restrict_of`, `static_admissibleFutureOn_restrict_of`). Instances: `E_κ` and Minkowski (`minkowski_exists_admissible_past_future`) (T-ett-measures-ealpha).
