title: A model-free BTZ-like point: the endpoint of the closed half-line
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-20
---
`Causal.closedHalfLine` on `Ici (0 : ℝ)` is causally arc-connected and its endpoint has `I⁻ = ∅ ≠ I⁺`,
a one-sided point without E₀ or any model import (`closedHalfLine_oneSided`). Its relations are
written with "every point between x and y lies in U", turned into an interval of ℝ by
`closedHalfLine_between_iff` (`Causal.LocateExtension`, T-abs-locate-extension).
