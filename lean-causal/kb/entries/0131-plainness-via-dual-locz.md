Causal plainness of a model whose future chronology fails on a thin set: apply Causality.causallyPlain_of_locZ to C.dual (LocZplus of C.dual is C's chronal past neighbours, LocZminus of C.dual off S is C's chronal future neighbours off S), then dual_causallyPlain; S needs only empty interior (Causal.notchedCausality_causallyPlain, S = the notch's edge).
