`Causal.ForkPoint` (a generic point `x`, two points `p`, `q` in its closure that cannot be separated; topology generated by `{{x}, {x,p}, {x,q}}`) with `LineDevelopment.allRelated` is a three-point non-Hausdorff spacetime: `toQ` leaves `{x, p}` at `1` and is still future-extensible there through `p` (`forkPoint_leaving_futureExtensible`). Use it to show a T2 hypothesis is needed (T-abs-schw-causal-lemmas).
