kind: pattern

`Causal.LineDevelopment.allRelated X`: every two points of an open set are related, both `≤` and `≪`; every continuous map is a chronal curve of it (`allRelated_chronalCurveOn`). With the circle `ℝ/Pℤ` (`circle_isTranslationAtlas`, `circle_isLineDevelopment`) it witnesses a periodic inextensible causal line (`circle_line`), which is why `BTZAxioms.line` asks a closed embedding only when `M` is causal (T-ett-line-development).
