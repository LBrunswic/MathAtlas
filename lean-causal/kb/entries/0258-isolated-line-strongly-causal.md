The isolated line (kb/0050) is strongly causal: every order-convex set of ℝ is causally convex in `M` (`isolatedLine_causallyConvexIn_Icc`, `isolatedLine_stronglyCausal`). It is causally open and not tarentin, so it is a cheap witness for theorems that take strong causality and causal openness without tarentin (T-abs-sc-open-without-tarentin).
