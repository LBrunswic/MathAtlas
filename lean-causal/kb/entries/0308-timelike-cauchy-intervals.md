On the timelike line, `{0}` is a Cauchy surface of every bounded open interval `]a, b[ ∋ 0`, symmetric or not: `Causal.timelikeLine_isCauchySurfaceOn_Ioo_of_neg_of_pos`, through `x ↦ m + k·arctan(x + x₀)` with `arctan x₀ = -m/k`; the same holds of every bounded order-connected open set `∋ 0` (`Causal.timelikeLine_isCauchySurfaceOn_of_ordConnected`, `Causal.Ers482Solution`). Use them when a witness's domains are not centred.
