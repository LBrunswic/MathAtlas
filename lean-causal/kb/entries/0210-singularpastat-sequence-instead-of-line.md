title: At a BTZ point, SingularPastAt's sequence stands in for the line when no causality is available
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-22
---
The first clause of `SingularPastAt w` gives u n → w, u n ≤ w, u n ≠ w. It replaces the BTZ line when only points of J⁻(w) ∖ {w} near w are needed, and asks for no closed embedding, hence no causality; if u n ∉ S₀, Z⁻ at u n takes over (T-ett-diamant-past).
