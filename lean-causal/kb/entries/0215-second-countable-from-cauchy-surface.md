`Causal.SingularRowsSecondCountable.secondCountableTopology_of_isCauchySurface` needs only [T2Space M] [ConnectedSpace M], IsoChartsCoverDEC25 and S Cauchy with SecondCountableTopology S: no global hyperbolicity, no absolute minimality. `secondCountable_of_cauchy` is A2's field verbatim, `_of_isAtlasCausalityDEC25` its natural-causality form. It turns a Cauchy surface's countability into M's, so the A2 facts that take [SecondCountableTopology M] apply. Every singular manifold in the library is already second countable by instance, so witnesses are vacuous in spirit (T-ett-sc-cauchy-e0).
