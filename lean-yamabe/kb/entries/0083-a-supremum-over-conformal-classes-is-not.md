Found by T-yamabe-thm-lower-bound. a topological invariant that is a supremum over conformal classes (σ^∂(Σ), σ(Σ̄)) is not definable on the model, because R and H are free data, so the supremum is unbounded and Real.sSup is junk 0. Carry it as a real parameter, state the sign-free form, and carry only the sign the paper's proof uses (Yamabe.MeasuredModel.gapBdry).
