Found by T-perf-capacity-remarks. pattern: cheap graph models for the measured model live in Yamabe.CapRemarks (scaffold CapacityRemarks). finModel k n mub e (Fin k, counting measure, flat frame finMetric, val = tr = id, constant differential e) comes with vol, bvol, gradSq, energy, dirichlet and volMean lemmas. edgeMap i j i' j' gives two edges. normSq_count_two and normSq_smul_dirac compute the norms. Disconnected, balanced and asymmetric-boundary examples cost about 30 lines each.
