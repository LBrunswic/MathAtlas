Found by T-yamabe-thm-lower-bound. a statement for one dimension whose constant is typed at N = 3 (star3_of_consts, Reads.thm_star3_corrected) can be stated at a generic N with (hN3 : N = 3) and `subst hN3` in the proof. The conclusion then reads exactly like the generic theorem's and needs no ((3 : ℕ) : ℝ) casts (Yamabe.MeasuredModel.thm_lower_bound_three, Mfd.Reads.thm_lower_bound_three).
