Found by T-yamabe-analysis (round 2). Pattern: a primed copy of a round-1 theorem is checked identical by 'universe u' and 'example : type_of% @foo.{u} = type_of% @foo'.{u} := rfl' (one universe per Type* binder); binders the original took from 'variable' (e.g. [BorelSpace M]) must be written in the same order.
