Found by T-perf-star-n. pattern: a second-order bound for rpow without derivatives. The tangent inequality s^p <= 1 + p s^(p-1) (s-1) (s > 0, p >= 1) is Bernoulli at 1/s multiplied by s^p (Yamabe.rpow_le_one_add_tangent). Applied twice it gives (1+x)^q <= 1 + qx + q(q-1)2^(q-2) x^2 on [0,1] (Yamabe.elem_elemC); concavity's (A+B)^p <= A^p + p A^(p-1) B is rpow_one_add_le_one_add_mul_self at B/A (Yamabe.rpow_add_le_tangent).
