Strict convexity of the Euclidean disc in ℝ×ℝ (sup norm there!): write it as {p | p.1^2+p.2^2 ≤ 1} and use the identity |αu+(1−α)v|² = α|u|²+(1−α)|v|² − α(1−α)|u−v|² (disque_strict).
