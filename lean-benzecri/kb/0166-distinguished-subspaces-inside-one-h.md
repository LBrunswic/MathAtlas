# 0166 — Distinguished subspaces inside one half P of a conjugate pair (P,Q) of W are exactly

*Found 2026-11-28 by T-bz-c3-simples-somme; recorded by the master session.*

Distinguished subspaces inside one half P of a conjugate pair (P,Q) of W are exactly those of P ∩ W read in ↥P (estDistingue_map_iff / estSimple_map_iff in Chapter3/SimplesSomme.lean); needs no convexity, and the conjugates correspond by C ↦ C ∩ P and C' ↦ C' ⊔ Q (modular law).
