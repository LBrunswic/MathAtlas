# 0020 — A convex body in every dimension; dimension 0 is vacuous for interiors

*Found 2026-11-21 by T-bz-c2-prop2-literal; recorded by the master session.*

`Benzecri.stdCorpsConvexe n` (the rays of |v_i| ≤ v_0, `isCorpsConvexeS_stdCorpsConvexe`) is a convex
body of S(n) in every dimension; for n ≥ 1 its interior has two distinct points
(`exists_ne_corpsInterior_iff`). The interior of a convex body of S(0) is one point
(`subsingleton_corpsInterior_dim_zero`) and l_V ≡ +∞ there, so a statement about A ≠ B in such an
interior is vacuous at n = 0, and « real-valued » fails there (OQ-45).
