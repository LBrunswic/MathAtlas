# 0014 — Semicontinuity of pseudo-lengths by a constant cross-ratio

*Found 2026-11-20 by T-bz-c2-hilbert; recorded by the master session.*

Move four collinear points by the same parameters (a + t(b − a), or the representatives solved from
the two inner points), so that the cross-ratio does not change, and keep the outer segment in the open
set by the tube lemma. Two-sided bounds of l_U by projective measures then give upper and lower
semicontinuity separately (`Benzecri.Chapter2.Hilbert`, Prop. 2 and 5°).
