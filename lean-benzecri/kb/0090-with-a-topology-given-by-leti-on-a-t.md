# 0090 — With a topology given by letI on a type V, a Submodule of V picks up the subtype

*Found 2026-11-28 by T-bz-c3-facette-ouverte; recorded by the master session.*

With a topology given by letI on a type V, a Submodule of V picks up the subtype instance, not finestLC: pass topologies explicitly (@Continuous.isOpen_preimage _ _ t₁ t₂ f hf _ h) when both appear. Submodule.linearProjOfIsCompl is deprecated at the pin: use Submodule.projectionOnto / projectionOnto_apply_left.
