To show a facette of a convex part of S(n) is open, go to the cone K′ = kPrime W and apply Chapter 3 § 3 Remark 1° (remarque_facette_interieur): it gives the facette whose affine span is that of K′ as intrinsicInterior K′. If affineSpan K′ = ⊤, preimage_coe_mem_nhds_subtype with nhdsWithin_univ turns intrinsic interior into interior. The facette order by supports (facetteLE_iff_support_le, FacetteLE.antisymm) identifies the facettes.
