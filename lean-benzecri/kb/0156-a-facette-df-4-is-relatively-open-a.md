# 0156 — A facette (Déf. 4) is relatively open: a closed convex body Wᵢ of a summand is a facette

*Found 2026-11-28 by T-bz-c3-somme-facettes-coniques; recorded by the master session.*

A facette (Déf. 4) is relatively open: a closed convex body Wᵢ of a summand is a facette of a closed sum only when Wᵢ is its own relative interior (isFacetteK_iff_relInterior_eq); read « Wᵢ facette » as « relint Wᵢ facette of closure Wᵢ ».
