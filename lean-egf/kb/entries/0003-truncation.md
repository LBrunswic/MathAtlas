title: From L²(λ̂) decay of densities to TV decay without comparing λ and λ̂: truncate first
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-20
---
Found by T-egf-headline. To turn the decay of d((φλ)πⁿ)/dλ measured against λ̂ into total-variation decay, truncate the density to min(f, K, K·dλ̂/dλ) before pushing it forward (`EGF.exists_truncation`, `EGF.exists_tvMass_pushIter_lt_of_tendsto`). No bound on dλ̂/dλ or its inverse is needed.
