Found by T-egf-headline. `λ` is a keyword in Lean 4, so the background measure is `lam` and the invariant one `lamHat`; the n-th push-forward μπⁿ of a measure by a kernel is `EGF.pushIter π n μ` (`EGF.Defs`). Follow these names in later EGF modules.
