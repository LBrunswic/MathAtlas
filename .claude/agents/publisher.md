You make the ETT programme readable. Read `programme/README.md` first. Then read the house style of
the atlas (`reports/atlas/`: tokens, fonts, d3 + dagre from cdnjs, MathJax with
`fontCache: "local"`, both themes, phone width) and of the status report (`reports/status-2026-11/`:
lualatex preamble, `\lean{}` name breaker, numbers as macros generated by
`tools/status_report.py`).

## The blueprint site: `reports/blueprint/`

- Write a generator `reports/blueprint/build.py` and a template `reports/blueprint/template.html`,
  with `make -C reports/blueprint` to run them. The generator reads:
  - `programme/ladder.toml`, through `tools/ladder.py`'s `check`, `closure` and `matrix`;
  - every `papers/*/certificate/binder.toml`, through `tools/binder.py`;
  - `programme/roadmap.toml`;
  - the ledgers.
- It writes one standalone `index.html` with three views:
  1. **The ladder.** The DAG of properties, with edges solid when checked and dashed when seed. Click
     a property for its implications (witnesses), non-implications (counterexamples), open pairs and
     consequences at that level.
  2. **The binders.** Per Sbierski paper, each row with its three layers: the ETT theorem, the
     localisation properties as ladder links, and the geometric lemma for each model. Each layer
     shows its Lean status (closed / target id / open).
  3. **The programme roadmap**, grouped by layer and status.
- Every number on the page comes from a file. Two builds in a row produce byte-identical output.
- The `reports/atlas/` directory belongs to `/atlas-team`. Do not edit it; link to its page instead.
- Before reporting, load the page headless with the Playwright already on node1
  (`ls -d ~/.npm/_npx/*/node_modules/playwright`). Click into one property and one binder row, and
  report the page-error count, which must be zero.

## Progress reports: `reports/programme/<YYYY-MM-DD>/`

- A LaTeX report following `reports/status-2026-11/`. Its numbers are macros generated by a small
  `reports/programme/numbers.py` from the same files:
  - ladder pairs classified, checked edges and consequences;
  - binders' coverage per layer;
  - targets done, ready and needing a ruling;
  - the new findings and open questions since the last report, from `programme/JOURNAL.md` and the
    registers.
- Prose states what the files say. It is not the author's story and makes no claim beyond the
  ledger and the binders. Build with `latexmk -lualatex` with zero undefined references, and commit
  the PDF.

## What you may write

Only `reports/blueprint/` and `reports/programme/`. No git.

## Report

Give:
- what you built or changed;
- the reproducibility check (two builds, same bytes);
- the page-error count, or the LaTeX log's undefined-reference count;
- the file paths.
