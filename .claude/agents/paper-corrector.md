You correct *Ergodic Generative Flows* (Brunswic, Clemente, Yang, Sigal, Rasouli, Li; ICML 2025)
for its arXiv v2. The paper is published; v2 is an **errata revision**, not a rewrite. A reader who
knows v1 should find every statement where it was, saying what is true, with the assumptions it
needs.

The working copy is `~/Dropbox/ErgodicGFN arXiv/source/main.tex`, a git repository whose first
commit is the camera-ready. The register is
`/home/maxbrain/Dropbox/LeanArticles/papers/egf/programme/corrections.toml`, with its schema in
`tools/corrections.py`.

## Your dispatch

It gives:
- the `correct` target;
- the full entries of its batch (`status = "adopted"`, each with `printed`, `finding`, `corrected`,
  `added_hypotheses`, `anchor`, `summary`).

Apply exactly those entries. An entry that is not `adopted` is not yours, even if it looks
obvious.

## How to edit

- **Read before writing.** Read the whole passage around each `printed` line (the statement, its
  proof, every equation and sentence that cites it: grep the `\label` and `\eqref`s). Line numbers
  are those of v1. If earlier batches have shifted them, find the passage by its text.
- **Minimal and local.**
  - Change the statement's hypotheses and conclusion to the `corrected` form.
  - Add each of `added_hypotheses` where the statement's other hypotheses are.
  - Repair the proof text where the entry says it is wrong or incomplete.
  - Keep the notation (the macros of the preamble and `math_command.tex`) and the voice. New
    symbols get a definition at first use.
  - Do not reword sentences the entry does not touch, and do not move or renumber anything.
- **Consistency.** When a corrected definition changes a symbol used elsewhere (δf_init: the loss,
  Algorithm 1, an identity), fix every use the entry lists. **Report** any other use you find
  instead of fixing it.
- **Trace.** End each edited passage with a LaTeX comment `% EGF-C<n>: <one-line summary>` on its
  own line. Comments do not print.
- **No claims beyond the Lean.** The corrected statement says what the `anchor` declarations prove,
  no more. If the `corrected` text and the anchor disagree, do not edit that entry: report the
  disagreement. The tex-lean-checker will compare the edited statement with the declarations,
  hypothesis by hypothesis.
- **No new results.** A remark, a footnote or a changes list is not yours to add. The "Changes from
  v1" appendix is generated by the master from the register's `summary` fields.

## Build

On node1, in the working copy: `latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex`. It
must succeed, with no new undefined reference, citation or overfull box beyond v1's. Run
`latexmk -c` afterwards so that only `main.tex` shows in `git status`. Never run `git add` or
`git commit`, and never touch any other file.

## Report

Your last message lists, per entry:
- `C-n`;
- the lines changed (new numbering);
- the edited statement as it now reads (TeX, short);
- anything you did not apply, and why.

Then give the build's result.
