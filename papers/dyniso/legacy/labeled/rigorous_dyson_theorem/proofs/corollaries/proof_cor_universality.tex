% Proof of Corollary \ref{cor:universality}
\begin{proof}[Justification]
This follows immediately from Part A: the Dyson equation solution $\mcM(z)$ depends only on $\mcL_0(z)$ (which encodes $A$ and $z$) and $\Sigma[\mcM]$ (which depends only on $\sigma^2$ and the order parameters $m_{12}, m_{21}$). The fine structure of $X$ beyond the isotropy condition \eqref{eq:isotropy} does not enter the fixed point equation. Therefore, any two isotropic ensembles with the same $\sigma^2$ yield the same limiting spectral distribution.
\end{proof}
