\begin{lemma}[Symmetry of Block Linearization]\label{lem:symmetry}
The block linearization matrix $\mcL(z) = \begin{pmatrix} zI & -Y \\ -Y^\top & I \end{pmatrix}$ is symmetric. Consequently, its resolvent $\mcR(z) = \mcL(z)^{-1}$ is symmetric, and the order parameters satisfy $m_{12} = m_{21}$.
\end{lemma}
