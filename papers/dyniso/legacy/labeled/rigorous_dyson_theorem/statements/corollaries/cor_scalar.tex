\begin{corollary}[Scalar Skip Connection]\label{cor:scalar}
If $A = aI$ for some $a \in \R$, then the $2N \times 2N$ Dyson equation reduces to a $2 \times 2$ scalar system. Specifically, $M_{ij} = m_{ij} I$ for all blocks, and the order parameters satisfy:
\begin{equation}
\begin{pmatrix} m_{11} & m_{12} \\ m_{21} & m_{22} \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} z & -(a + \sigma^2 m_{12}) \\ -(a + \sigma^2 m_{21}) & 1 \end{pmatrix}^{-1}.
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}
