\begin{corollary}[Spectral Universality]\label{cor:universality}
The limiting spectral distribution $\mu$ depends only on the following data:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The norm $\norm{A}$ and spectral structure of $A$
\item The variance parameter $\sigma^2$
\item The two order parameters $(m_{12}, m_{21})$
\end{enumerate}
In particular, the fine structure of $X$ beyond the isotropy condition \eqref{eq:isotropy} does not affect the limiting spectrum.
\end{corollary}
