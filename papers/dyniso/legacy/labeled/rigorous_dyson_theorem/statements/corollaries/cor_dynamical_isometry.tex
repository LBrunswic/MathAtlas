\begin{corollary}[Achieving Dynamical Isometry]\label{cor:dynamical-isometry}
For a residual network with $a = 1$ (identity skip connections), dynamical isometry is achieved when
\begin{equation}
c = \frac{1}{2},
\end{equation}
independent of the depth $L$ or the choice of activation function $\phi$ (up to its effect on $c$).
\end{corollary}
