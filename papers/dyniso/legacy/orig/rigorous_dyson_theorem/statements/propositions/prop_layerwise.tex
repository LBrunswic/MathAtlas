\begin{proposition}[Layerwise Spectral Analysis]\label{prop:layerwise}
Assume:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W^l_{ij} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_w^2/N)$ i.i.d.
\item The pre-activation distribution at layer $l$ has concentrated empirical measure $\rho^l$
\item The activation function $\phi$ is differentiable with $|\phi'| \leq C$
\end{enumerate}

Then the matrix $D^l W^l$ is approximately isotropic with effective variance
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{eff}^{2,l} = \frac{\sigma_w^2}{N} \E_{h \sim \rho^l}[\phi'(h)^2].
\end{equation}

The singular spectrum of $Y^l = aI + D^l W^l$ is governed by the Dyson equation with order parameters $(m_{12}^l, m_{21}^l)$.
\end{proposition}
