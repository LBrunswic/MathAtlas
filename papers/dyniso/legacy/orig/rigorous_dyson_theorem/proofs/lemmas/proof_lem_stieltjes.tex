% Proof of Lemma \ref{lem:stieltjes}
\begin{proof}
Standard results from complex analysis and measure theory. Items (1)-(3) follow from the integral representation and Cauchy's integral formula. Item (4), the Stieltjes inversion formula, is the key uniqueness result connecting the transform to the measure. For complete proofs, see \cite{bai2010spectral} Chapter 2, Theorem 2.1, or \cite{anderson2010introduction} Section 2.1.
\end{proof}
