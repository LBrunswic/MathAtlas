% Proof of Corollary \ref{cor:dynamical-isometry}
\begin{proof}
Setting $a = 1$ in equation \eqref{eq:universal} and seeking a spectrum concentrated near $\lambda = 1$, we set $G(z)$ near the pole at $z = 1$. Expanding around this point with $c = 1/2$ yields
\[
G(z) \approx \frac{1}{z - 1} + O(1),
\]
which corresponds to a Dirac mass at $\lambda = 1$.

For $c \neq 1/2$, the spectrum spreads away from $1$, degrading gradient flow properties.
\end{proof}
