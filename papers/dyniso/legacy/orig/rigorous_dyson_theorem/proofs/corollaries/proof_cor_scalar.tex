% Proof of Corollary \ref{cor:scalar}
\begin{proof}
When $A = aI$ is a scalar multiple of the identity, the block matrix $\mcL_0(z) = \begin{pmatrix} zI & -aI \\ -aI & I \end{pmatrix}$ commutes with the self-energy $\Sigma[\mcM] = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & \sigma^2 m_{12} I \\ \sigma^2 m_{21} I & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. Thus, the unique solution $\mcM(z)$ from Part A must also have blocks proportional to identity: $M_{ij} = m_{ij} I$ for scalars $m_{ij}$. The $2N \times 2N$ Dyson equation reduces to a $2 \times 2$ matrix equation for the order parameters $(m_{11}, m_{12}, m_{21}, m_{22})$, which can be solved explicitly by block matrix inversion.
\end{proof}
