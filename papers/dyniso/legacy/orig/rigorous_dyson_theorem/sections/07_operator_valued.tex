\section{Operator-Valued Free Probability for Kronecker Skip Connections}\label{sec:ov-free-prob}

The Hyper-Connection skip $A_q \otimes I_p$ (Section~\ref{sec:kronecker-skip}) has non-trivial eigenvalue structure when $A_q$ is bistochastic or orthogonal, and the scalar Dyson equation cannot resolve the individual spectral sectors. This section develops the \emph{operator-valued} (matrix-valued) generalization over the base algebra $\mcB = M_q(\C)$, which is what makes the spectral comparison of Section~\ref{sec:bistochastic-vs-ortho} quantitatively precise. We state results without proof, following~\cite{voiculescu1995operations,speicher1998combinatorial,dykema2006multilinear}; see~\cite{mingo2017free} for a comprehensive treatment.

\subsection{Kronecker collapse to a $q \times q$ fixed-point}\label{sec:matrix-dyson-theory}

The key insight is that the Kronecker structure of $A_q \otimes I_p$ respects the block decomposition of $M_N(\C)$ into $q \times q$ blocks of size $p \times p$. The conditional expectation $E_\mcB = \mathrm{id}_q \otimes \tr_p$ (the normalized partial trace over $p$-fibers) extracts the $q \times q$ block structure, and the isotropy of $W$ ensures the self-energy has no off-diagonal $p \times p$ structure. The result is a collapse from an $N$-dimensional to a $q$-dimensional fixed-point.

\begin{proposition}[Kronecker collapse]\label{prop:kronecker-collapse}
Under the Kronecker structure~\eqref{eq:kronecker-skip} and the isotropy assumption on $W$, the $\mcB$-valued order parameter $M(z) \in M_q(\C)$ satisfies the self-consistent equation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:matrix-dyson}
M(z) = A_h(M) \cdot \bigl(zI_q - A_h(M)^\top A_h(M)\bigr)^{-1},
\end{equation}
where $A_h(M) := A_q + \sigma^2 M$ is the $q \times q$ dressed matrix. The $\mcB$-valued Cauchy transform is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G-B}
G^{(\mcB)}(z) = \bigl(zI_q - A_h(M)^\top A_h(M)\bigr)^{-1},
\end{equation}
and the scalar Cauchy transform is $G(z) = \frac{1}{q}\Tr_q(G^{(\mcB)}(z))$. When $q = 1$, the scalar theory of Sections~\ref{sec:num-dyson}--\ref{sec:z1-mapping} is recovered exactly.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Operator-valued S-transform and Dykema's twisted multiplicativity}\label{sec:ov-s-transform}

For multi-layer composition with $q > 1$, the S-transform generalises to a $\mcB$-valued map. The $\mcB$-valued S-transform is $S^{(\mcB)}(W) := G^{(\mcB)}(b) \cdot W^{-1}$, where $b$ solves the \emph{Psi-inversion}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:psi-inversion}
b \cdot G^{(\mcB)}(b) - I_q = W.
\end{equation}
This inherits a \emph{twist} from the non-commutativity of $\mcB$:

\begin{theorem}[Dykema, 2006]\label{thm:dykema}
Let $x$ and $y$ be $\mcB$-freely independent positive elements. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:twisted-mult}
S^{(\mcB)}_{xy}(W) = S^{(\mcB)}_y(W) \cdot S^{(\mcB)}_x\!\bigl(S^{(\mcB)}_y(W)^{-1} \, W \, S^{(\mcB)}_y(W)\bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\noindent For $L$ layers, this gives the twisted recursion:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:twisted-fold}
S_{\mathrm{cumul}} \leftarrow S^{(\mcB)}_L(W); \quad \text{for } k = L{-}1, \ldots, 1: \;\; W_{\mathrm{tw}} = S_{\mathrm{cumul}}^{-1} W S_{\mathrm{cumul}}, \;\; S_{\mathrm{cumul}} \leftarrow S_{\mathrm{cumul}} \cdot S^{(\mcB)}_k(W_{\mathrm{tw}}).
\end{equation}
When $q = 1$, the twist is trivial (scalars commute) and~\eqref{eq:twisted-fold} reduces to $S_{\mathrm{cumul}} = \prod_l S_l(w)$.

\subsection{Multi-layer spectrum recovery}\label{sec:ov-multilayer}

The $L$-layer $\mcB$-valued Cauchy transform is obtained by solving the consistency equation: find $W \in M_q(\C)$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:outer-consistency}
(W + I_q)\bigl(S_{\mathrm{prod}}(W) \cdot W\bigr)^{-1} = z I_q,
\end{equation}
where $S_{\mathrm{prod}}(W)$ is the twisted product~\eqref{eq:twisted-fold}. The scalar density is then $G_L(z) = \frac{1}{qz}\Tr_q(W + I_q)$.

\subsection{Dispatch and identical-vs-heterogeneous layers}\label{sec:identical-vs-hetero}

When all layers share the same $A_q$ and $\sigma^2$, the scalar $z_1$-mapping (Proposition~\ref{prop:z1-mapping}) applied to $G_1(z) = \frac{1}{q}\Tr_q(G^{(\mcB)}_1(z))$ produces the exact $L$-layer scalar Cauchy transform, because identical operator-valued S-transforms commute on $\{wI_q\}$. For non-commuting twist matrices, however, the scalar product deviates from the projection of the twisted product~\eqref{eq:twisted-fold}, and the full pipeline is required (Appendix~\ref{app:ov-pipeline}, Figure~\ref{fig:hetero-panel}). The method is selected as follows:
