\begin{remark}[Optimality]
The rate $O(1/N^2)$ is optimal for the $L^2$ error of empirical spectral measures of random matrices. The $\eta^{-2}$ dependence is also sharp, as resolvents necessarily blow up as $z$ approaches the real axis.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Comparison with classical results]
Classical results for Wigner matrices (e.g., \cite{bai2010spectral}) give rates of $O(1/N)$ for the convergence of individual eigenvalues. Our $O(1/N^2)$ rate for the Green's function reflects the additional smoothing from the trace operation and the stability properties of the Stieltjes transform.
\end{remark}
