## What it bears on in `../../NOTES-3+1.md` [r]

These are the planner's readings, not the paper's.

- **N4, Conjecture G's « Physics context *[to verify]* ».** N4 says that Carroll, Farhi and Guth
  (1992) and Deser, Jackiw and 't Hooft (1992) « show that such a total cannot arise from timelike
  constituents in an open universe ».
  - Quote 6 says the opposite of those words: two subluminal (timelike) cosmons *do* have a
    tachyonic total.
  - What DJH show is quote 7: a boost-identified spacetime is never obtained from a
    rotation-identified one, so Gott's CTCs come in from spacelike infinity. Quotes 2 and 9 make
    that a boundary condition at spacelike infinity which they call unphysical.
  - The statement about subsystems is CFGO's (`../cfgo1994/NOTE.md`, quote 5). If the total
    momentum of an open universe is timelike, then no spacelike subsystem can arise.
  - The correction is recorded in `../../NOTES-3+1.md`, N10.
- **N5, Theorem G's hyperbolic row (« a Gott pair »).** Quotes 4–5: Gott's pair is the case where
  the composite identification is boostlike. That is Theorem G's hyperbolic P_in. DJH get CTCs
  there only from the boundary condition at infinity. Theorem G's outcome under BBS's causality
  hypotheses is a BTZ-like singularity instead. The two do not conflict: the hypotheses differ.
- Cutler, Phys. Rev. D 45 (1992) 487 (quote 8) is not archived.
