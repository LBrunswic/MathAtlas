# P10 — double-run with defect $w = x^{q}\,y\,z^{p}$

**Verdict: no-useful-compensation ✗ (certified obstruction) for $x\neq z$; reduces to P2 ✔ only on the merge locus $x=z$.**

P10 generalizes both P0 ($p=0$, tail $w'=y$) and P2 ($x=z=(d)$, $y=(e)$). The two-stage telescoping
predicted in `PATTERNS.md` works exactly as advertised — each run telescopes to a $q$- (resp. $p$-) free
core — but the **combination of the two combinations cannot be assembled inside a single word's rotation
sums**, and the residual carries an unremovable $3$-power ($Y$) growth. So when $x\neq z$ the two runs do
*not* merge and no finite $q,p$-free core bounds $G$. P2 wins precisely because $x=z$ collapses the two
runs into one defect-circuit.

## Setup (certified against the kernel)

Write $a=Y^{o_x},A=X^{E_x},s_x=S(x)$; $b=Y^{o_y},B=X^{E_y},s_y=S(y)$; $c=Y^{o_z},C=X^{E_z},s_z=S(z)$.
Folding the cocycle through $M(x)^qM(y)M(z)^p$ gives (identity **(S)**, verified for all blocks, $q,p\in[0,3]$):
$$S(w)=c^{p}b\,s_x\,G_x(q)+c^{p}A^{q}s_y+A^{q}B\,s_z\,G_z(p),\quad G_x(q)=\tfrac{A^q-a^q}{A-a},\ G_z(p)=\tfrac{C^p-c^p}{C-c}.$$
All $q,p$-growth lives in $A^q,C^p,G_x,G_z$.

## Combination — both stages

**Stage (i) — telescope the $x$-run** (P0 block-difference; identity verified $q\in[1,3],p\in[0,3]$):
$$\Delta_x \;=\; S(w)-S\!\big(R^{|x|}w\big) \;=\; X^{(q-1)E_x}\,C_x(p),\qquad
C_x(p)=C(x,\,yz^p)=D(x)S(yz^p)-D(yz^p)S(x).$$
$q$-free, but $C_x(p)$ still depends on $p$.

**Stage (ii) — telescope the $z$-run** (rotate $w\sim z^px^qy$, then block-difference; verified):
$$\Delta_z \;=\; X^{(p-1)E_z}\,C_z(q),\qquad C_z(q)=D(z)S(x^qy)-D(x^qy)S(z).$$
$p$-free, but $C_z(q)$ still depends on $q$.

**The combined picture (identity (D), the heart of the matter).** Both cores split *exactly* along
their geometric growth modes, with $q,p$-free block coefficients:
$$\boxed{\;C_x(p)=\frac{X^{E_y}\,C(x,z)\,X^{pE_z}\;-\;N\,Y^{po_z}}{D(z)},\qquad
C_z(q)=\frac{-\,N\,X^{qE_x}\;+\;Y^{o_y}\,C(x,z)\,Y^{qo_x}}{D(x)}\;}$$
where $C(x,z)=D(x)S(z)-D(z)S(x)$ is the $x,z$ block-pair core and
$$N=X^{E_x+E_y}s_z-X^{E_x+E_z}s_y+X^{E_x}Y^{o_z}s_y-X^{E_y}Y^{o_x}s_z+X^{E_z}Y^{o_x}s_y-X^{E_z}Y^{o_y}s_x-Y^{o_x+o_z}s_y+Y^{o_y+o_z}s_x$$
is the three-block numerator. Equivalently $C_x(p)$ satisfies the 2nd-order recurrence
$C_x(p)-(X^{E_z}+Y^{o_z})C_x(p-1)+X^{E_z}Y^{o_z}C_x(p-2)=0$ — characteristic roots $X^{E_z},Y^{o_z}$.

## Core $C$ and its $X$-exponent vs $E$

For $x\neq z$ off the shared-fixed-point locus, $C(x,z)\neq 0$, so $C_x(p)$ genuinely carries:
- leading $X$-term of exponent $E_y+pE_z$ — **grows with $p$** (a $2$-power, strippable by odd $G$);
- trailing $Y$-term of exponent $p\,o_z$ — **grows with $p$** (a $3$-power, **not** strippable by odd $G$).

These two modes are independent functions of $p$. A $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$-combination of $C_x,C_z$ that is
$q,p$-free and nonzero would force *both* mode coefficients to vanish: $C(x,z)=0$ **and** $N=0$.

The "kill-the-$Y$-mode" trick $C_x(p)-Y^{o_z}C_x(p-1)=X^{E_y}C(x,z)\,X^{(p-1)E_z}$ *does* leave a pure
$X$-power times the $q,p$-free $C(x,z)$ — but it mixes the cores of two *different* words ($z^p$ and
$z^{p-1}$), so it is **not** a combination of rotation sums of the single word $w$, and is therefore not
divisible by $w$'s gcd $G$. A direct search over all rotation-pair differences of a single $w$ finds the
minimal-$X$-degree difference saturates in $X$ but carries a factor $Y^{2(p-1)}$ whose odd-part value
grows by $3^{o_z}$ per step — confirming the $3$-power obstruction is intrinsic, not an artifact.

## GCD bound

**None for $x\neq z$ off the degenerate locus.** Every available single-word rotation combination retains
unremovable $Y^{p o_z}$ growth, so no fixed $C$ satisfies $G\mid C$ for all $p$ — and indeed $|D(w)|$
grows without bound while $G=|D|$ on a cycle. This is the same geometric/imprimitivity obstruction that
killed P3/P4/P9: telescoping one run leaves the *other* run's geometric factor undivided.

On the **merge locus $x=z$** the two runs fuse: $d^q e\,d^p$ is cyclically $d^{q+p}e$, a single-defect
circuit, and $C(x,z)=C((d),(d))=0$ automatically. P2's escape applies, giving
$$|2^{E}-3^{o}|\;\le\;2^{\,|e-d|}-1\qquad(\text{independent of }q,p).$$

## Degenerate locus $C\equiv 0$

$C(x,z)=0$ means $x,z$ share the rational fixed point $r=s_x/D(x)=s_z/D(z)$; then (certified)
$N=D(x)D(z)\,(D(y)\,r-s_y)$, so $N=0$ additionally forces $s_y=D(y)\,r$ — i.e. **$x,y,z$ all share the
single fixed point $r$**. On that stratum both $C_x$ and $C_z$ vanish identically. It is exactly the
imprimitive / single-circuit (bounded-circuit) locus (Steiner; Simons–de Weger), where the cheap ceiling
is vacuous. The merge $x=z$ ($\Rightarrow C(x,z)=0$) is the boundary case that recovers P2.

## Caveats / battery results

- **Symbolic identities (the proofs):** (S) closed form; (i) $\Delta_x$ telescoping; (ii) $\Delta_z$
  telescoping; (D) exact two-mode decomposition of $C_x,C_z$; (O) the obstruction $C(x,z)=0\Rightarrow
  N=D(x)D(z)(D(y)r-s_y)$ and full-degenerate collapse — all **PASS** in $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ over the block
  set $\{(1,2)(3)(2,1),\ (2)(5)(4),\ (1,1,2)(4)(2,3),\ (3)(1)(2),\ (1)(2)(3)\}$, $q,p$ in the ranges noted.
- **Divisibility battery** at $(2,3)$: $G\mid\Delta_x$ over $80$ instances ($5$ block triples $\times\,q,p\in[1,4]$).
  **PASS**, no failures. (This only re-confirms $\Delta_x$ is a legitimate rotation difference; it does
  *not* certify a bound, since $\Delta_x$ grows with $p$.)
- **P2 reduction:** verified $x=z\Rightarrow C(x,z)=0$ and the P2 core divisibility holds.
- **Open end:** the obstruction is for combinations of a single word's rotation sums. A genuinely
  *different* device (e.g. relating $w$ at consecutive $p$ via an external recurrence, à la a Matveev
  bound on the family) is outside the cocycle-combine remit and not claimed here.
