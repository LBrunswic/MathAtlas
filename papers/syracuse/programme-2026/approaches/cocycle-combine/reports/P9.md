# P9 — Balanced blocks $w = u_1 u_2 \cdots u_q$

**Verdict:** ✗ no useful single-word compensation for $q\ge3$ — a certified
circulant/geometric obstruction blocks any $q$-uniform core. At $q=2$ the only clean
extraction is the P0 antisymmetry core, which (empirically) forces $s_1=s_2$, i.e.
collapse to P0.

Each block has length $\operatorname{len}(u_i)=o$ and gap-sum $|u_i|=E$. Write
$a:=Y^o$, $b:=X^E$, $s_i:=S(u_i)$. The common diagonal gives the structural collapse
$$S(w)=\sum_{i=0}^{q-1} a^{\,q-1-i}\,b^{\,i}\,s_{i+1},\qquad
D(w)=b^q-a^q=(b-a)\sum_i a^{q-1-i}b^i,\quad D(u_i)=b-a.$$
A block-rotation $R^k$ (cyclic shift of the *blocks*) is the letter-rotation by $k\cdot o$,
and
$$S(R^k w)=\sum_{i=0}^{q-1} a^{\,q-1-i}\,b^{\,i}\,s_{(k+i)\bmod q}.$$

## The combination space is circulant

Writing $S(R^k w)=\sum_j A_{kj}\,s_j$, the matrix $A$ is **circulant** with generator
row $c_m=a^{\,q-1-m}b^{\,m}$ (i.e. $A_{kj}=c_{(j-k)\bmod q}$). Hence **every**
$\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$-linear combination $\sum_k g_k\,S(R^k w)$ has core coefficient vector
$$\mathrm{core}_j=(g\circledast c)_j\qquad(\text{circular convolution}),\quad g_k\in\mathbb{Z}[X,Y].$$

## Positive results (certified)

- **Symmetric identity (lead 1):**
  $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}S(R^k w)=\Big(\sum_i a^{q-1-i}b^i\Big)\Big(\sum_j s_j\Big)
  =\frac{D(w)}{D(u)}\sum_j s_j.$ Certified for $q=2..6$.
- **$q=2$ antisymmetry core (collapse to P0):**
  $$S(u_1u_2)-S(u_2u_1)=(a-b)(s_1-s_2)=(Y^o-X^E)\big(S(u_1)-S(u_2)\big).$$
  Certified abstractly and via `verify_symbolic_identity` on all distinct balanced pairs
  for $(o,E)\in\{(2,3),(2,5),(3,5)\}$.

## The obstruction for $q\ge3$ (certified)

In $R=\mathbb{Z}[X,Y][t]/(t^q-1)$, the generator polynomial $c(t)=\sum_m c_m t^m$ satisfies
$$c(t)\,(b\,t-a)\equiv b^q-a^q=D(w)\pmod{t^q-1}\quad(\text{a constant}),$$
so $c(t)$ is a **unit** (after inverting $D(w)$); the reachable cores are exactly $c(t)\cdot R$.
The generator is **geometric**, $c_m=a^{q-1}(b/a)^m$, and its top $X$-power term is $c_{q-1}=b^{q-1}$.
In $\mathrm{core}_j=(g\circledast c)_j$ the $b^{q-1}$ contribution comes from the **unique**
index $k=(j+1)\bmod q$ as $g_k\,c_{q-1}$; since every other monomial in the convolution has a
strictly different $(a,b)$-bidegree, this top term **cannot cancel**. Therefore every nonzero
$\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$-combination has $X$-degree $\ge E\,(q-1)$ — **growing with $q$**. Brute force
(integer and monomial coefficients) confirms the minimum $X$-degree is exactly $q-1$ for
$q=2,3,4$. This is the P3-style geometric obstruction: the geometric weight is unavoidable and
$q$-dependent. The genuinely $q$-free extraction (multiply a difference by $(bt-a)$ to telescope
the geometric factor) mixes words of different block-counts and lives in **P4** (cross-length),
outside P9's single-word remit.

## Combination & Core $C$

| | Combination | Core $C$ | $X$-degree |
|---|---|---|---|
| $q=2$ | $S(u_1u_2)-S(u_2u_1)$ | $(Y^o-X^E)(S(u_1)-S(u_2))$ | $\le E+(E-1)$, $q$-free |
| $q\ge3$ | any $\sum_k g_k S(R^k w)\ne0$ | $\mathrm{core}=g\circledast c$ | $\ge E(q-1)$, **grows with $q$** |

## GCD bound

Only at $q=2$. On a genuine cycle $G=\gcd_j S^{(j)}=|D(w)|=|b^2-a^2|=|D(u)|\,(2^E+3^o)$;
the factor $|D(u)|=|a-b|$ divides the core $(a-b)(s_1-s_2)$, leaving the residual ceiling
$$(2^E+3^o)\ \big|\ (s_2-s_1).$$
The block-sum spread $\max_i s_i-\min_i s_i$ stays well below $2^E+3^o$ for small $E$
(e.g. $(o,E)=(2,3)$: spread $2$ vs ceiling $17$), so the ceiling forces $s_1=s_2$.
For larger $E$ relative to $o$ the raw spread can exceed $2^E+3^o$ (e.g. $(4,8)$: spread $570$
vs $337$), yet an **escape search** over $(o,E)$ up to $(5,9)$ found **no** distinct balanced
pair with $(s_1-s_2)$ a nonzero multiple of $2^E+3^o$. So empirically $q=2$ **always collapses
to P0** ($s_1=s_2$). For $q\ge3$ no usable cycle ceiling is produced (core $X$-degree grows).

## Degenerate locus

$C\equiv0\iff s_1=s_2$ (equivalently all block-sums equal). This is precisely the **P0
stratum**: equal block-sums with the common diagonal force $S(R^kw)$ to coincide up to the
geometric weight, recovering $w^q$-type behaviour and the known bounded-circuit core.

## Caveats / battery results

- Symbolic identities certified via `verify_symbolic_identity`: structural geometric formula
  and block-rotation = letter-rotation$(k\cdot o)$ on real words; symmetric identity ($q=2..6$);
  $q=2$ antisymmetry core (abstract + 82 real pairs); the unit relation
  $c(t)(bt-a)\equiv b^q-a^q$ ($q=2..7$); min $X$-degree $=q-1$ ($q=2,3,4$, brute force).
- Numeric battery at $(2,3)$: $q=2$ core, 82 balanced pairs over
  $(o,E)\in\{(2,3),(2,5),(3,5),(2,7),(3,6)\}$ — 0 failures; general-$q$ adjacent-difference
  core, 12 words ($q=2..5$) — 0 failures. Note these use $G=\gcd$ over **all** letter-rotations
  (the kernel's `gcd_rotations`), which is the necessary cycle condition; on non-cycles $G$ may
  be $1$ and the check is trivially satisfied.
- Escape search for $(2^E+3^o)\mid(s_1-s_2)$, $s_1\ne s_2$, over
  $(o,E)$ up to $(5,9)$: 0 escapes. (Empirical, not a proof that none exist for all $E,o$ — an
  open end: a transcendence-flavoured statement would be needed to rule out escapes uniformly.)
