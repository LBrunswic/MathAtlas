# P1 — single circuit / constant-gap word $d^o$

**Verdict:** ∅ degenerate / pipeline warm-up. The compensation exists and is certified, but the
core equals the gcd itself, so the resulting ceiling is **tight but vacuous** as a finiteness
lever. This is the single-circuit ($C=0$-style) stratum, classically finite (Steiner 1977).

## Pattern

The constant word $w = (d,d,\dots,d)$ with $o$ letters (every gap equals $d$). Then
$E = |w| = do$ and $\operatorname{len} w = o$.

## Combination

No nontrivial linear combination of rotations is needed. All $o$ cyclic rotations of $d^o$ are
the **same** word, so $S^{(0)}=S^{(1)}=\dots=S^{(o-1)}=S(d^o)$. The single "combination" is the
geometric collapse via the cocycle:
$$
(X^d - Y)\,S(d^o) \;=\; D(d^o) \;=\; X^{do} - Y^{o},
\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad
S(d^o) \;=\; \frac{X^{do} - Y^{o}}{X^d - Y} \;=\; \sum_{k=0}^{o-1} (X^d)^{\,k}\,Y^{\,o-1-k}.
$$
This is exactly the partial-sum definition $S = \sum_k X^{e_k}Y^{o-1-k}$ with $e_k = kd$ — a
geometric series in $X^d$, hence factors through $D$.

## Core $C$

$$
C \;=\; S(d^o) \;=\; \frac{X^{do} - Y^{o}}{X^d - Y}.
$$
Its $X$-exponent (gap-sum degree) is $d(o-1)$, which **grows with the family parameter $o$** — it
does *not* compensate to a parameter-free constant. At $(2,3)$, $C = (2^{do}-3^o)/(2^d-3) = D/(2^d-3)$.

## GCD bound

Because every rotation sum is equal, the shift-gcd is the sum itself:
$$
G \;=\; \gcd_{0\le j\lt o} S^{(j)}(d^o) \;=\; |S(d^o)| \;=\; \left|\frac{2^{do}-3^o}{2^d-3}\right|
\;=\; \frac{|D|}{|2^d-3|}\quad(d\ne\log_2 3).
$$
The a-priori ceiling $G \mid C$ therefore holds with **equality**, $C = G$. The nominal bound
$|2^{do}-3^o| \le |C|\cdot|2^d-3|$ is just the definition of $G$ rearranged — it imposes **no
upper bound** on $|2^E-3^o|$ and kills no words. Matches the PATTERNS.md goal $G = |D|/|2^d-3|$
exactly.

## Degenerate locus

$C = S(d^o) = 0$ requires $X^{do} = Y^o$ as polynomials, which has no positive-integer
specialization $(X,Y)=(2,3)$ (and at the polynomial level only the trivial collapse). So $C$ is
nonzero throughout the family at $(2,3)$; there is no proper degenerate sublocus to exclude.

## Caveats / battery results

- **Symbolic identity:** `verify_symbolic_identity((X**d - Y)*S(w), D(w))` passes for all
  $d\in\{1..5\}$, $o\in\{1..6\}$ (30 instances); geometric closed form verified identically.
- **Rotation degeneracy:** confirmed all rotations identical and $G = |S(d^o)| = |D/(2^d-3)|$
  over the same 30 instances.
- **Divisibility battery:** `verify_divisibility_battery` with `core_fn = S_int` passes over
  $d\in\{1..6\}$, $o\in\{1..8\}$ (48 instances). Trivially so, since $C = G$.
- **Interpretation:** This row validates the kernel + certifier pipeline end-to-end. As a
  finiteness statement it is vacuous: the constant word is one circuit repeated, the classical
  single-circuit case (Steiner; Simons–de Weger), and the cocycle machinery only re-derives
  $G$. Useful compensations must come from *non-constant* words where rotations differ and a
  genuine $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ combination cancels the parameter-dependent leading terms.
