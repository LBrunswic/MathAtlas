# P3 — Two-block periodic $(uv)^q$

**Verdict:** ✗ no useful compensation — reduces to the P0 core, with a certified
structural obstruction blocking any single-word improvement.

Period word $A=uv$ (two blocks $u,v$); $\tau=A^q$. Write $|A|=\sum A$ (gap-sum),
$\operatorname{len}A$ (number of letters), $B=vu$. Set
$$C(u,v):=S(uv)-S(vu)=D(u)S(v)-D(v)S(u).$$

## The naive reading is vacuous

"$\tau=(uv)^q$ is P0 with $w=uv,\ w'=\varnothing$" gives core
$C(uv,\varnothing)=D(uv)S(\varnothing)-D(\varnothing)S(uv)=0$ since
$S(\varnothing)=0$ and $D(\varnothing)=X^0-Y^0=0$. So the baseline P0 instance is
degenerate; the real question is whether the inner $u\mid v$ boundary buys anything.

## Structural obstruction (the heart of the result)

$\tau=A^q$ is a perfect $q$-th power, hence **imprimitive** for $q\ge2$. Its distinct
cyclic rotations are exactly the $\operatorname{len}A$ inner rotations of the period,
each raised to the $q$-th power: $\operatorname{rot}(\tau,i)=A_i^{\,q}$ with
$A_i=\operatorname{rot}(A,i)$. Iterating the cocycle (the P1 closed form for a periodic
block) gives, **for every** $i$,
$$S(A_i^{\,q})=S(A_i)\cdot\operatorname{geo}_q,\qquad
\operatorname{geo}_q=\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}X^{k|A|}Y^{(q-1-k)\operatorname{len}A}=\frac{D(A^q)}{D(A)}.$$
The factor $\operatorname{geo}_q$ is **independent of $i$** (it depends only on
$|A|,\operatorname{len}A$). Hence **every** $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$-linear combination of
rotation sums of $\tau$ factors through it:
$$\sum_j c_j\,S^{(j)}(\tau)=\operatorname{geo}_q\cdot\Big(\sum_i c_i'\,S(A_i)\Big).$$
$\operatorname{geo}_q$ is **not** a pure power of $X$ (it mixes $X$ and $Y$), and its
$X$-degree is $(q-1)|A|$, which **grows linearly in $q$**. So $G$ (odd) cannot strip it,
and no single-word combination of rotations of $(uv)^q$ can reach a $q$-free core. The
smallest $q$-free core attainable is at $q=1$, where $\operatorname{geo}_1=1$.

## Combination & core

- **$q=1$ reduction (no improvement).** $S(uv)-S(vu)=C(u,v)=D(u)S(v)-D(v)S(u)$ — exactly
  the P0/antisymmetry core with $(w,w')=(u,v)$. The "iterate $u\leftrightarrow v$" idea
  yields nothing beyond this single core; both orderings give $\pm C(u,v)$.
- **Cross-length $q$-free identity (P4 territory, *not* single-word).** The
  $\operatorname{geo}_q$ factor *can* be telescoped away by a finite difference in $q$ that
  mixes words of different lengths ($A^q$ and $A^{q-1}$), which are **not** rotations of one
  word:
  $$\big[S(A^q)-S(B^q)\big]-Y^{\operatorname{len}A}\big[S(A^{q-1})-S(B^{q-1})\big]=C(u,v)\cdot X^{(q-1)|A|}.$$
  This *is* a clean $q$-free core $C(u,v)$ up to a pure $X$-power — but it lies in the P4
  cross-length program, not in P3's single-word remit.

## Core $C$ and its $X$-exponent

Best single-word $q$-free core: $C(u,v)$, identical to P0/P2. Its $X$-exponent is
$\le|A|-1=|uv|-1$ (one period), **independent of $q$** but **not smaller** than the
naive single-period P0 core. No joint core below $C(u,v)$ is pinned.

## GCD bound

**None usable.** For $q\ge2$, $(uv)^q$ is imprimitive, so it is never the gap word of a
genuine Syracuse cycle; the mechanism $G\mid C$ (which constrains only cycles) does not
apply, and indeed $G\nmid C(u,v)$ numerically for $q\ge2$ (see battery). For $q=1$ the
word is the single period $uv$ and the bound is the ordinary P0 ceiling
$|2^{|uv|}-3^{\operatorname{len}(uv)}|\le|C(u,v)|$ — no gain over P0.

## Degenerate locus

$C(u,v)\equiv0\iff D(u)S(v)=D(v)S(u)$, i.e. $uv$ and $vu$ have equal sum functionals
(includes $u=v$ and the shared-fixed-point stratum). This is the same $C=0$ locus as P0.

## Caveats / battery results

- Symbolic identities certified over $u,v\in\{(1,2),(2,),(1,1,3),(3,2),(1,),(2,1),(1,4),(1,3)\}$
  and $q\in[1,5]$: rotation factorization $S^{(j)}=S(A_i)\operatorname{geo}_q$, the
  "combination inherits $\operatorname{geo}_q$" claim, the $q=1\to$ P0 core identity, and the
  cross-length finite-difference identity — all pass.
- Battery at $(2,3)$: $G\mid C(u,v)$ holds at $q=1$ (it *is* the P0 core); for $q\ge2$ there
  are 24 documented non-divisions across the test grid — **expected**, since those words are
  imprimitive (not cycles) and $G=\gcd_jS^{(j)}$ grows like $D(A^q)$. We report these as
  evidence of vacuity, not as a failed bound.
- Open end: the genuine $q$-free extraction belongs to **P4** (finite-difference in $q$), where
  it is worth pursuing as a real result; within P3's single-word scope it is unreachable.
