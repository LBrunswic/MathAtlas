# P4 — finite-difference in $q$, family $\tau_q = w^q w'$

**Verdict:** ✗ **no-useful-compensation** — and, more precisely, a **confirmation of P0, not a
sharpening.** The finite-difference operator in $q$ does collapse the recurrence to an *exact
closed form for $S(w^qw')$*, but the resulting "core" is **not** a $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$-combination
of cyclic rotations of a single word, so the divisibility lemma $G\mid(\cdot)$ does not apply and
**no gcd ceiling follows from it.** The only gcd-valid extraction from the recurrence is exactly
the P0 within-word difference, which reproduces the P0 core $C(w,w')$ verbatim.

## Pattern

Same eventually-periodic family as P0: $\tau_q=w^qw'$ with $w$ a block of length $n=\operatorname{len}(w)$,
gap-sum $|w|$, and tail $w'$ of length $n'=\operatorname{len}(w')$. Treat $f(q):=S(w^qw')$ as a
sequence in $q$ and apply the proposed shift annihilator $(\sigma_q-X^{|w|})$, $\sigma_q:q\mapsto q-1$.

## The recurrence (certified)

Splitting off the leading block with the cocycle $S(uv)=Y^{\operatorname{len}v}S(u)+X^{|u|}S(v)$:
$$
f(q) \;=\; X^{|w|}\,f(q-1) \;+\; Y^{(q-1)n+n'}\,S(w),\qquad f(1)=S(ww').
$$
This is a **first-order linear $q$-recurrence** whose inhomogeneity $Y^{(q-1)n+n'}S(w)$ is itself
geometric in $q$ with ratio $Y^{n}$.

## The proposed combination and why it gives no ceiling

The PATTERNS-row operator collapses the recurrence to its inhomogeneity:
$$
\big(\sigma_q-X^{|w|}\big)f \;=\; f(q)-X^{|w|}f(q-1) \;=\; Y^{(q-1)n+n'}\,S(w). \tag{$\ast$}
$$
This is the proposed "exact minimal core". It is fatal for the cocycle-combine program on **two**
independent grounds:

1. **It is not a rotation combination.** $f(q)$ and $f(q-1)$ sum the words $w^qw'$ and $w^{q-1}w'$,
   which have **different lengths** and are therefore *not cyclic rotations of one word*. The
   governing lemma — "$G(\tau)=\gcd_jS^{(j)}(\tau)$ divides every $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$-combination of
   the rotations of $\tau$" — simply does not apply. The numeric battery confirms this directly:
   over 24 family instances with $G(\tau_q)>1$, the integer value of $(\ast)$ is divisible by
   $G(\tau_q)$ in only **1/24** cases (a coincidence), i.e. **$G\nmid(\ast)$ generically.**
2. **It carries a growing power of $Y\to3$.** Even setting aside (1), $(\ast)=Y^{(q-1)n+n'}S(w)$ is
   a power of $Y$ (which $\to 3$) times $S(w)$. $G$ is odd but is **not** coprime to powers of $3$,
   and the exponent **grows linearly in $q$** — the opposite of the parameter-free core the
   approach seeks.

## Exact closed form (the genuine by-product)

The full **homogeneous** annihilator is second-order, with characteristic roots $X^{|w|}$ and $Y^{n}$:
$$
\big(1-Y^{n}\sigma_q\big)\big(1-X^{|w|}\sigma_q\big)\,f \;=\;
f(q)-\big(X^{|w|}+Y^{n}\big)f(q-1)+X^{|w|}Y^{n}f(q-2)\;=\;0,
$$
certified for all tested families. Solving the inhomogeneous recurrence gives the exact closed form
$$
\boxed{\,S(w^qw') \;=\; X^{(q-1)|w|}\,S(ww') \;+\; S(w)\,Y^{\,n'+n}\,
\frac{X^{(q-1)|w|}-Y^{(q-1)n}}{D(w)}\,}
$$
(equivalently $X^{(q-1)|w|}S(ww')+S(w)Y^{n'}\sum_{k=2}^{q}X^{(q-k)|w|}Y^{(k-1)n}$). This is an
exact formula for $S$ itself — it is *not* a divisibility/ceiling statement, and its $X$-exponent
$(q-1)|w|$ grows with $q$.

## The only gcd-valid extraction = P0

The recurrence does contain the P0 ceiling, but it lives in the **within-word** difference of two
genuine rotations of the *same* word $\tau_q$ (rotate $\tau_q$ left by $n$ letters: $\tau_q^{(n)}=w^{q-1}w'w$):
$$
S^{0}(\tau_q)-S^{n}(\tau_q) \;=\; X^{(q-1)|w|}\,C(w,w'),\qquad
C(w,w') \;=\; D(w)\,S(w')-D(w')\,S(w),
$$
certified for all tested families. Since $G$ is odd it ignores the $X^{(q-1)|w|}$ prefactor, so
$G\mid C(w,w')$ — exactly the P0 result. The numeric battery confirms $G\mid C(w,w')$ over all
tested instances.

## Core $C$ and GCD bound

$$
C \;=\; C(w,w') \;=\; D(w)\,S(w') - D(w')\,S(w),
\qquad |2^{E}-3^{o}| \;\le\; |C(w,w')|.
$$
$C$ is **$q$-free**; its $X$-exponent (gap-sum degree) is $|w|+|w'|$, independent of $q$. This is
**identical to P0** — P4 supplies no new or smaller core.

## Degenerate locus

$C(w,w')=0$, i.e. $D(w)S(w')=D(w')S(w)$ — the shared rational-fixed-point stratum (bounded-circuit
core, Steiner / Simons–de Weger). Same locus as P0.

## Caveats / battery results

- **Symbolic identities (all PASS)**, certified over families $w\in\{(1,2),(2,1,1),(3),(1,1),(1),
  (2),(1,2),(2,3),(1,1,2)\}$ with matching tails, $q=2..6$:
  the first-order recurrence, the collapse $(\ast)$, the second-order homogeneous annihilator
  $Pf=0$, the exact closed form, and the P0 within-word identity.
- **Obstruction battery:** 24 instances with $G(\tau_q)>1$ (from $w\in\{(1),(2),(1,2),(2,1),(1,1,2),
  (2,3)\}$, tails $\{(1),(2),(3),(1,2),(2,1)\}$, $q=2..6$); the $\sigma_q$ combination $(\ast)$ is
  $G$-divisible in only $1/24$ — confirming it yields **no** valid ceiling.
- **P0 core battery:** $G\mid C(w,w')$ passes on every tested instance (the genuine, P0-confirmed
  ceiling).
- **Honest summary:** the finite-difference idea is mathematically correct (the recurrence and
  closed form are exact and pretty), but the operator steps *outside* the rotation orbit of a
  single word, where the gcd lemma has no purchase. The only piece that stays within the orbit is
  the P0 block-rotation difference. **P4 = a confirmation of P0, not a sharpening.**
