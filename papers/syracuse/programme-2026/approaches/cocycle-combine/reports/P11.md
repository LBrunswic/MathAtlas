# P11 — stratum twist / topological cover

**Verdict: ✗ no covering gain (clean negative, with two certified positives).**

The increment identities (1) and the unit-mod-$D$ fact (2) are **certified** and recover
P2 exactly. But the central P11 claim — that reusing one (or a few) certificate $(a,b)$ gives
a *topological cover* of a whole stratum with a **small, controlled** core — **does not hold**.
With the canonical certificate $b=1$ (so $C(w)\equiv S(w)\bmod D$), one certificate does cover
every non-cycle word (it is nonzero mod $D$), but the residue **scatters across the entire
interval $(0,|D|/2]$** and a single twist moves it by order $|D|$. There is **no uniform small
ceiling** beyond the vacuous $|C|<|D|$. P11 is, after the dust settles, a restatement of the
word-by-word fact "$D\mid S(w)$ iff $w$ is a cycle candidate" — no covering leverage.

## Setup

Fix the stratum $\mathcal S_{E,o}=\{$gap words = compositions of $E$ into $o$ positive parts$\}$.
Every $w\in\mathcal S_{E,o}$ has $D(w)=X^E-Y^o$, i.e. $D(2,3)=2^E-3^o$, **independent of $w$**.
The lever is the two transfer entries $D,S$ directly: a certificate is $a,b\in\mathbb Z[X,Y]$ with
$$C(w):=a\,D+b\,S(w),\qquad 0<|C(w)(2,3)|<|D|.$$
On a genuine cycle $D=\gcd_j S^{(j)}\mid S(w)$, so $D\mid b\,S(w)$ and $D\mid a\,D$, hence
$D\mid C(w)$; with $|C(w)|<|D|$ that forces $C(w)=0$, a contradiction — so $w$ is not in a cycle.
(Divisibility logic confirmed against the kernel: `gcd_rotations` $=|D|$ on cycles.)

## (1) Increment identities — certified in $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$

With $S(w)=\sum_{k=0}^{o-1}Y^{\,o-1-k}X^{P_k}$, $P_k=d_0+\dots+d_{k-1}$, only one partial sum
moves under a local twist. Both identities pass `verify_symbolic_identity` over a battery of
explicit words and all interior positions $i$:

- **Adjacent transposition** ($d_i\leftrightarrow d_{i+1}$, $0$-based):
  $$S(w')-S(w)=Y^{\,o-2-i}\,X^{P_i}\,\bigl(X^{d_{i+1}}-X^{d_i}\bigr),\qquad P_i=d_0+\dots+d_{i-1}.$$
- **Unit transfer** ($d_i\to d_i+1,\ d_{i+1}\to d_{i+1}-1$):
  $$S(w')-S(w)=Y^{\,o-2-i}\,X^{P_{i+1}}\,(X-1),\qquad P_{i+1}=d_0+\dots+d_i.$$

Both preserve $E$ and $o$, so $D(w')=D(w)$ and the certificate transfers:
$C(w')=C(w)+b\,(S(w')-S(w))$.

## (2) Unit-mod-$D$ fact, odd-part size, P2 recovery — certified

- $\gcd(2^E-3^o,\,6)=1$ over $E\in[2,17],\,o\in[2,\min(E,10)]$: **PASS**. ($2^E-3^o$ is odd; and
  $\equiv(-1)^E\not\equiv0\pmod 3$.)
- Hence the monomial prefactor $Y^{o-2-i}X^{P_i}\to 3^{o-2-i}2^{P_i}$ is a **unit mod $D$**, and
  the essential (unit-stripped) size of the increment at $(2,3)$ is the odd part of
  $X^{d_{i+1}}-X^{d_i}$, namely $2^{|d_{i+1}-d_i|}-1$: **PASS**.
- **P2 recovery**: the swap across the single defect of the background circuit $d^o$ (rotations
  $d^{o-2}e\,d$ vs $d^{o-1}e$) has increment $X^{(o-2)d}(X^e-X^d)$, odd part $2^{|e-d|}-1$ — exactly
  P2's ceiling. **PASS**. Divisibility battery $D\mid(S^{(0)}-S^{(1)})$ over 36 P2 words: **PASS**.

So the *single increment* is genuinely small ($X$-exponent $|d_{i+1}-d_i|$, $a,b$-free) — P11's
increment **is** the P2 ceiling, now for a swap inside any background word.

## (3) The stratum experiment — the real question, answered

**Combination / propagation rule.** Canonical certificate $b=1$, $a$ absorbing $D$-multiples, so
$C(w)\equiv S(w)\pmod D$ taken as the least-magnitude residue in $(-|D|/2,|D|/2]$. Propagation:
$C(w')\equiv C(w)+(b\,u)\,(X^{d_{i+1}}-X^{d_i})\pmod D$, $u$ a unit monomial.

Enumerating whole strata (script output):

| $(E,o)$ | $|D|$ | #words | uncovered ($C\equiv0$) | $|C|$ range over covered | $|D|/2$ | single-twist $|\Delta C|$ mean |
|--------|------|-------|----------------|------------------|--------|------------------|
| (5,3)  | 5    | 6     | 0              | [1, 2]           | 2      | 1.8 |
| (8,5)  | 13   | 35    | 0              | [1, 6]           | 6      | 3.4 |
| (7,4)  | 47   | 20    | 0              | [2, 23]          | 23     | 12.8 |
| (10,6) | 295  | 126   | 0              | [4, 147]         | 147    | 82.7 |
| (11,7) | 139  | 210   | **7**          | [1, 69]          | 69     | 42.8 |
| (12,7) | 1909 | 462   | 0              | [2, 953]         | 954    | 532.0 |

**Findings (honest):**

1. **Coverage is total but trivial.** With $b=1$, one certificate makes $C(w)\ne0\bmod D$ for
   *every* word except those with $S(w)\equiv0\bmod D$. So a single certificate "covers" the
   whole stratum minus the zero-set. But this coverage is **not a covering theorem** — it is the
   tautology $C(w)\equiv S(w)$, and $C\ne0$ is just $D\nmid S(w)$.

2. **The uncovered words are exactly the genuine cycle candidates.** Across a wide sweep
   ($E\le19$), the words with $S(w)\equiv0\bmod D$ are precisely (i) the periodic circuits $d^o$
   and block-periodic words (the P1 / bounded-circuit stratum), the trivial $|D|=1$ words, and
   (ii) at $(E,o)=(11,7)$, $D=-139$, the **7 rotations of $(1,1,1,2,1,1,4)$** — for which *all*
   rotations vanish mod $D$, i.e. $\gcd_j S^{(j)}=|D|=139$, the exact cycle-candidate signature.
   The script asserts every uncovered word has $\gcd_{\text{rot}}=|D|$. So the zero-set is *not*
   coverable by changing $(a,b)$ in a way that helps — these are the words cycle-gcd must rule out
   by size, and any certificate with $b$ a unit mod $D$ has $C(w)\equiv0$ there too.

3. **No small ceiling.** The covered residues fill $(0,|D|/2]$ essentially uniformly: $\max|C|$
   reaches $|D|/2$ in every stratum (script asserts $\max|C|\ge|D|/4$). The bound $|C|<|D|=|2^E-3^o|$
   is **vacuous** — it is exactly the a-priori interval, giving no information.

4. **The propagation radius is $\sim|D|$, not small.** Although the increment $X^{d_{i+1}}-X^{d_i}$
   is small as a polynomial (and unit-stripped odd part $2^{|d_i-d_{i+1}|}-1$ is the P2 size), once
   multiplied by the unit monomial $u=3^{o-2-i}2^{P_i}$ and **reduced mod $D$**, the step
   $u\,(2^{d_{i+1}}-2^{d_i})\bmod D$ is a generic residue: mean $|\Delta C|$ runs $\sim|D|/3$ to
   $|D|/2$ (e.g. 532 of 954 at $(12,7)$). A single twist therefore throws the residue across the
   whole interval — the "radius" of a covered base word is $O(1)$ twists, useless for tiling.

**Why the small increment doesn't survive.** The increment is small *as an element of
$\mathbb Z[X,Y]$* (low $X$-exponent), but the lever multiplies it by a **unit mod $D$** and reduces
mod $D$. Reduction mod $D$ destroys the size of the polynomial: a unit times a small bracket is a
generic residue. P2 wins because it never reduces mod $D$ — it bounds the *integer* $C$ directly by
its $X$-exponent ($2^{|e-d|}-1$, away from $|D|$). P11's mod-$D$ framing throws that away.

## Core $C$

$$C(w')\equiv C(w)+b\,(S(w')-S(w))=C(w)+b\,Y^{o-2-i}X^{P_i}(X^{d_{i+1}}-X^{d_i})\pmod D.$$
As an integer (least residue), $|C|$ is uniformly distributed over $(0,|D|/2]$; its $X$-exponent
is meaningless after reduction. The single-increment $X$-exponent is $|d_{i+1}-d_i|$ (the P2 value),
but it is not preserved mod $D$.

## GCD bound

P11 delivers **only** the vacuous $|2^E-3^o|\le|C|<|2^E-3^o|$ from the mod-$D$ residue — no useful
ceiling. The genuinely small bound it contains is **P2's**, recovered as the special case of a
defect swap: $|2^E-3^o|\le 2^{|e-d|}-1$ when one twist isolates a single defect against an otherwise
constant background. P11 does **not** extend this to a stratum-wide covering bound.

## Degenerate locus $C\equiv0$

$C(w)\equiv0\bmod D \iff D\mid S(w)$. The zero-set is exactly: periodic/block-circuit words (P1
stratum), $|D|=1$ words, and the genuine cycle candidates where **all** rotations vanish mod $D$
($\gcd_j S^{(j)}=|D|$). At $(11,7)$ this is the orbit of $(1,1,1,2,1,1,4)$. These are precisely the
words P11 cannot cover with any $b$-unit certificate — the bounded-circuit + cycle-candidate locus.

## Caveats / battery results

- **Symbolic identities (1):** PASS — adjacent transposition and unit transfer, over explicit
  words and all interior $i$ (the proof).
- **Unit-mod-$D$ + odd-part + P2 recovery (2):** PASS — $\gcd(D,6)=1$ over $E\in[2,17]$,
  $o\in[2,\min(E,10)]$; odd-part $=2^{|d_i-d_{i+1}|}-1$; P2 ceiling recovered; divisibility battery
  over 36 P2 words.
- **Stratum experiment (3):** ran clean over $(E,o)\in\{(5,3),(8,5),(7,4),(10,6),(11,7),(12,7)\}$.
  Uncovered words = genuine cycle candidates (asserted $\gcd_{\text{rot}}=|D|$). Covered $|C|$
  reaches $\ge|D|/4$ (asserted). No anomalies.
- **Open end:** a multi-certificate scheme (a *set* of $(a,b)$ whose union of small-$|C|$ regions
  covers the stratum) is not ruled out here, but the residue's near-uniform spread means each
  certificate covers only an $O(|D|^{-1})$ fraction with $|C|$ below any fixed bound — so the number
  of certificates needed to keep $|C|$ below, say, $|D|/k$ grows like $k$, i.e. you need $\sim|D|$
  certificates to get a non-vacuous bound. That is no better than word-by-word $D\mid S(w)$. **No
  covering gain.**
