- **Feeds:** reference map for the §5 barrier, the word/parity lineage, and the search (no new claim).

## What it says (relevant part only)

The authoritative annotated index. Entries that bear directly on this approach:

- **Cycle lower bounds.** Steiner 1978 (no nontrivial 1-cycle/circuit, via Baker; the exact ref is
  Congressus Numerantium XX, 553–559); Eliahou 1993 (period $=301994A+17087915B+85137581C$, min
  $\ge17{,}087{,}915$; CF of $\log_23$); Halbeisen–Hungerbühler 1997; **Simons–de Weger 2005** (Acta
  Arith. 117, 51–70: finitely many $m$-cycles each $m$; none for $m\le68$, later $\le75$; uses **Rhin
  1987**, *Progr. Math.* 71, 155–164); Sinisalo 2003 (min length $\ge630{,}138{,}897$); Brox 2000
  (finiteness of cycles with few descents, Baker–Feldman).
- **Word / parity-vector lineage.** Terras 1976 & Everett 1977 (the encoding vector); Huang–Wu 2000
  (bijection $\mathbb N\leftrightarrow$ parity vectors); Shallit–Wilson 1991 (parity sets are
  $2$-automatic / regular); **López–Stoll 2009** (*Integers* 9, A13: the $3x+1$ conjugacy map over a
  **Sturmian word**); **Bernstein–Lagarias 1996** (the conjugacy $\Phi:\mathbb Z_2\to\mathbb Z_2$ with
  $\Phi^{-1}T\Phi=$ shift).
- **Free-semigroup framing.** Misiurewicz–Rodriguez 2005 / Bergelson–Misiurewicz–Senti 2006: the
  semigroup generated by $x/2$ and $(3x+1)/2$ is **free on two generators** — independent corroboration
  of Trümper's free-semigroup picture ([[trumper2014-free-semigroup]]).

## How it connects here

A map, not a claim. Two takeaways. (1) **Our angle appears genuinely under-explored in the canon:** the
bibliography has *no* entry indexed under "Christoffel word", "transfer matrix", or "cocycle" for $3x+1$
— the closest are López–Stoll (Sturmian) and Stolarsky's Beatty-sequence symbolic dynamics. The
word-cocycle + Christoffel framing of `structure.md` (C1–C9) sits in that gap. (2) It pins the precise
references for the **§5 barrier** (Rhin 1987 is the sharp tool) and confirms the **free-semigroup**
structure of Trümper/§6 is independently known (Misiurewicz–Rodriguez).
