## What it says (relevant part only)

Trümper builds the free semigroup $\mathscr W$ of **T-words** $w=ud^{x_1}ud^{x_2}\cdots ud^{x_y}$
($y$ = number of $u$/odd steps, $x=\sum x_i$ = number of $d$/halvings; our $o=y$, $E=x$, gaps
$d_k=x_k$). He defines on it the **link function**
$L(w)=\sum_{k=1}^{y}2^{x_1+\dots+x_{k-1}}3^{y-k}$ (eq. 45/47) and $D(w)=2^x-3^y$ (eq. 50). The
**composition laws** (eqs. 53–56) are $x(w_aw_b)=x_a+x_b$, $y(w_aw_b)=y_a+y_b$,
$D(w_aw_b)=3^{y_b}D(w_a)+2^{x_a}D(w_b)$, $L(w_aw_b)=3^{y_b}L(w_a)+2^{x_a}L(w_b)$. The **commutator
relation** (eq. 75) is $L_{ab}-L_{ba}=D_aL_b-D_bL_a$. The **cycle function** $\Phi(w)=L(w)/D(w)$ is
the (rational) cycle start number; $\Phi$ integer $\iff$ cycle, and a one-step **rotation** acts by
$\Phi'=(3\Phi+1)/2^{x_1}$ for $D>0$ (eq. 82, Lemma 4) — the Collatz map itself. Repetition is
invariant: $\Phi(w^n)=\Phi(w)$ (Lemmas 2, 5; $D(w^n)=2^{nx}-3^{ny}$). Cycles classify by $(L,D)$ as
*trivial* ($L{=}D{=}1$), *simple* ($D{=}1,L{>}1$), *amazing* ($L,D>1$). Bounds for fixed $(x,y)$:
$3^y-2^y\le L\le 3^{y-1}+2^{x-y+1}(3^{y-1}-2^{y-1})$ (eq. 64), with the extremes at
$w_{\min}=(ud)^{y-1}ud^{x-y+1}$ and $w_{\max}=ud^{x-y+1}(ud)^{y-1}$.

## How it connects here

This is an **independent rediscovery of our entire §1–§4 representation**, in different notation, and
so is strong external validation. Dictionary: Trümper's $L\equiv S$ (link function = rotation sum =
Knight's $g$), his $D\equiv D$, his $\Phi=L/D$ = the cycle start number $S/D$. His composition law for
$L$ (eq. 56) is precisely the $(2,1)$-entry of our monoid hom **C1/C2** ($S(w_aw_b)=Y^{o_b}S(w_a)+
X^{E_a}S(w_b)$), and the parallel law for $D$ is the eigenvalue/diagonal entry; dividing by $Y^{o}$
gives the crossed-homomorphism **C5**. His commutator (eq. 75) is *literally* our wedge core **C7**
$C(u,v)=D_uS_v-D_vS_u$. His rotation law on $\Phi$ is **C3** acting on the cocycle, and his
repetition-invariance is the **P1** circuit degeneracy ($S=D/(X^d-Y)$). Everything is generic
$\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ (his "$x,y,L,D$ are diophantine functions of the T-word"); the only arithmetic input
is the sign/oddness of $D$. New vocabulary worth importing: *cycle function* $\Phi$, *amazing cycle*
($L,D>1$, the genuine target), the $3m\!+\!1$/$3m\!-\!1$ **duality** (same $L$; $D$ vs. $\bar D=3^y-2^x$
flips sign), and "**harmonic numbers**" $2^a3^b$ (Philippe de Vitry / Carlebach) for the terms of $L$ —
a direct line to the exceptional $2$–$3$ ($S$-unit) tier.
