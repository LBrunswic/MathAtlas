# Approaches

This file is the index of every approach we are pursuing toward the 3x+1 (Collatz / Syracuse) conjecture. Each approach has its own folder under `approaches/<name>/`. Keep entries here brief — full details live in the per-approach `RATIONALE.md` and `STATUS.md`.

See [`CHEATSHEET.md`](CHEATSHEET.md) for a proof-free quick reference of all formulas and partial results, each linked to its source derivation.

## Conventions

Per-approach folder layout:

```
approaches/<name>/
├── RATIONALE.md   # The idea, hypotheses, why it might work
├── STATUS.md      # Current state, results so far, dead-ends, next questions
└── …              # Code, notebooks, derivations
```

Each entry below has three lines:
- **Rationale** — one-sentence elevator pitch.
- **Status** — one phrase: `active`, `stalled — <reason>`, or `ruled out — <reason>`.
- **Results** — one or two bullets of the most load-bearing findings or dead-ends so far.

When a result, dead-end, or pivot lands, update both the approach's `STATUS.md` and the entry here.

### diophantine-distribution

- **Rationale** — Read $Z(u)=S(u)/D(u)$ as a fundamental solution attached to *any* word (via the conjugacy $Z\circ\mathrm{shift}=T\circ Z$ and the linear-Diophantine general solution $S(u)/D(u)+2k\,2^{|w|}$); factor it as $\eta/(\beta-3)$, $\beta=2^{E/o}$, with imbalance $\eta\in[2^{-p},2^p]$, and use word-geometry size bounds on the fundamental solution to prove the flying time $\max\{n:T^n(x)\ge x\}$ is finite for every positive integer.
- **Status** — active — fundamental-solution layer proved + machine-checked ([`fundamental.md`](approaches/diophantine-distribution/fundamental.md)); target shown equivalent to the conjecture; quantitative program (integrality amendment, polynomial denominator) open.
- **Results**
  - **Target ⟺ conjecture (proved):** flying-time finiteness for all positive integers implies Collatz by strong induction ($T^p(x)\lt x$ + all smaller reach $1$ ⟹ $x$ reaches $1$).
  - **FS1–FS2 (proved):** the word-map is affine $F_w(x)=\tfrac{3^o}{2^E}x+\tfrac{S(w)}{2^E}$ with unique fixed point $x_w^\ast=S(w)/D(w)\in\mathbb{Z}_2$ ($D$ odd); on a cycle this is the start, integer iff $D\mid S$. Reproduces $-1$, $-7$.
  - **FS3 (proved):** trajectories with word exactly $w$ and $a_o$ odd form one class $x_w^\ast+2^{|w|+1}\mathbb{Z}_2 = x_w^\ast+2k\,2^{|w|}\mathbb{Z}_2$ (Terras + word↔compressed-string). The pitch's factor of $2$ is exactly the extra bit certifying the word closes on an odd $a_o$.
  - **FS4–FS5a (proved):** $S(w)/D(w)=\eta/(\beta-3)$, $\beta=2^{E/o}$, with $\eta\in[2^{-p},2^p]$, $p=\max_k|e_k-\tfrac{E}{o}k|$ (balanced-word lemma); contracting+large ($\beta\ge\tfrac72$) ⟹ $0\lt x_w^\ast\le2^{p+1}$ independent of $E$. All checked on 2000+ words.
  - **Statistics, 500k odd starts $\le10^6$ ([`results/RESULTS.md`](approaches/diophantine-distribution/results/RESULTS.md)):** relative position $\rho=(x-x_w^\ast)/2^E$ of the flying-word fundamental solution tracks flying time (Spearman $-0.976$), imbalance $+0.919$ — but **largely mechanical** (flight length $E$ has $\rho_s=0.989$; partialling $E$ out drops them to $-0.16$/$-0.13$, and $|x-x_w^\ast|\sim x$ is flat). The non-mechanical survivor is criticality $E/o\to\log_2 3$ (partial $-0.393$). Use $E$-residualized statistics.

### cycle-gcd

- **Rationale** — Set $a_o=a_0$ in the closed form to get the cycle equation $(2^E-3^o)a_0 = \sum_k 2^{e_k}3^{o-1-k}$; a cycle is invariant under cyclic shift of its gap word, so $D:=2^E-3^o$ divides every shifted RHS sum — bound $\gcd_j S^{(j)}$ from above to force only finitely many admissible $(E,o)$, hence finitely many cycles.
- **Status** — active — structural results proved; first a-priori gcd bound in hand for eventually-periodic words $w^q w'$, now written up with an effective Matveev exclusion zone.
- **Results**
  - **Bourbaki article on $w=u^qv$ ([`eventually-periodic.tex`](approaches/cycle-gcd/eventually-periodic.tex), 8pp PDF):** self-contained — §1 the cocycle calculus (characters, concatenation cocycle, transfer-matrix hom, $\gcd_j S^{(j)}=|D|$); §2 the $u^qv$ case with **no a-priori hypothesis on $u,q,v$**: telescoping → $|2^E-3^o|\mid C(u,v)$, $q$-free $|C|<2^{a+b}3^{m+\ell}$, explicit Matveev $2^E-3^o>3^o e^{-c_0(1+\log E)}$ ($c_0\approx5.9\times10^8$), combined into $(q-1)m\log3<(a+b)\log2+c_0(1+\log E)$ ⟹ effective $q\le Q(u,v)$; critical band $3^o<2^E<3^{o+1}$ applied afterward to pin $E$ to ≤2 values per $o$.
  - **Eventually-periodic words $w^q w'$ ([`periodic-words.md`](approaches/cycle-gcd/periodic-words.md)):** in $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ a concatenation cocycle gives block-telescoping $S(w^qw')-S(w^{q-1}w'w)=X^{(q-1)|w|}C(w,w')$ with $q$-free core $C=D(w)S(w')-D(w')S(w)$. As $D$ is odd, $\gcd_j S^{j}\mid C(w,w')$ — an a-priori, $q$-independent ceiling — so a cycle forces $|2^E-3^o|\mid C$: finitely many $q$ per $(w,w')$ unless $C=0$ (= shared fixed point / pure-circuit case). Matveev meant to make it quantitative & uniform.
  - **Rotation recursion (algebraic, any word):** $2^{d_0}S(w') = 3S(w) + D$. Hence $\gcd_j S^{(j)} \mid D$ for every gap word.
  - **Exact gcd on cycles:** the cycle condition $D \mid S^{(j)}$ collapses with the above to $\gcd_j S^{(j)} = |2^E-3^o|$ exactly — so any upper bound on the gcd must be a priori (word-structure only), not via divisibility. Verified on the $1\to1$ and $-5\to-7$ cycles.
  - **Criticality lemma:** non-critical $(E,o)$ (i.e. $E\gt o\log_2 3+1$) satisfy $2^E-3^o\gt 2^{E-1}$; a positive cycle is critical $\iff 2^E\lt 2\cdot3^o$. The bound proves the lemma and reduces criticality to $2^E\lt 2\cdot3^o$, but does **not** close it — the size route fails (witness $(9,5)$, word $(1,2,2,2,2)$: $\min_j S^{(j)}=431\gt D=269$); closure needs the divisibility/gcd layer.

### cocycle-combine

- **Rationale** — Child of `cycle-gcd`: since on a cycle $\gcd_j S^{(j)}=|2^E-3^o|$ is odd, every $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$-linear combination $\sum_j c_j S^{(j)}$ of the rotation sums is divisible by it; an agentic search hunts, pattern-by-pattern, for combinations that *compensate* to a small core $C$, yielding the a-priori ceiling $|2^E-3^o|\le|C|$. Generalizes the proved $w^qw'$ telescoping to arbitrary word-patterns and combinations.
- **Status** — active — infrastructure scaffolded and the agentic loop validated end-to-end; first new ceiling (P2) certified; structural theory written + certified. Fanning combinators across the pattern menu is the work.
- **Results**
  - **Structural theory (certified)** ([`structure/structure.md`](approaches/cocycle-combine/structure/structure.md), owned by the `cocycle-structure` skill): the word-cocycle is a transfer-matrix monoid hom $M(w)=\big(\begin{smallmatrix}Y^o&0\\S&X^E\end{smallmatrix}\big)$; rotation = conjugation (cyclic words = conjugacy classes); the coboundary of a constant is $w\mapsto cD(w)$ (so $S$ is defined mod $\langle D\rangle$ — why odd $G$ ignores prefactors); the core is a wedge $C(u,v)=\det\big(\begin{smallmatrix}D(u)&D(v)\\S(u)&S(v)\end{smallmatrix}\big)$ obeying a Plücker/Jacobi relation $\sum_{\rm cyc}D(u)C(v,w)=0$. Organizing axis: generic $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ algebra vs. exceptional $2$–$3$ arithmetic. All 8 claims pass `verify_structure.py`.
  - **Pipeline built + kernel verified** ([`lib/cocycle.py`](approaches/cocycle-combine/lib/cocycle.py)): $S,D$, rotations, cocycle/antisymmetry identities, and two certifiers (`verify_symbolic_identity` over $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$, `verify_divisibility_battery` for $G\mid C$ at $(2,3)$); self-test reproduces the $1\to1$, $-5\to-7$ data. Patterns catalogued in [`PATTERNS.md`](approaches/cocycle-combine/PATTERNS.md), state in [`LEDGER.md`](approaches/cocycle-combine/LEDGER.md).
  - **P2 single-defect circuit $d^aed^b$ (new ceiling, certified):** the last-adjacent-rotation difference $S^{(o-2)}-S^{(o-1)}=X^{(o-1)d}(X^{e-d}-1)$ has odd part $2^{|e-d|}-1$, so any such cycle forces $|2^E-3^o|\le 2^{|e-d|}-1$ — **independent of the run multiplicities $a,b$**, hence finitely many per defect $(d,e)$. Certified ($\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ identity + 1512-batch + 864 indep. checks); non-vacuous ($(1,1,1,4,1,1,1)$: $|D|{=}1163$ vs bound $7$). Degenerate $\iff e=d$.
  - **P11 stratum twist / topological cover (✗, certified — increment real, cover fails):** a propagation idea — reuse one word's certificate $(a,b)$ across the fixed-$(E,o)$ stratum (shared $D$) via adjacent transpositions. The increment $S(w'){-}S(w)=Y^{o-2-i}X^{P_i}(X^{d_{i+1}}{-}X^{d_i})$ is certified, and since $\gcd(D,6){=}1$ its monomial is a unit mod $D$ with unit-stripped size $2^{\lvert d_i-d_{i+1}\rvert}-1$ = **the P2 ceiling exactly**. But the cover collapses: reducing mod $D$ turns the small increment into a generic residue (fills $(0,\lvert D\rvert/2]$), so one certificate gives only the tautology "$D\mid S(w)$ iff cycle candidate" — no leverage over `cycle-gcd` size-elimination. **Lesson: a ✔ must bound the *integer* core by its $X$-exponent and never reduce mod $D$** (which is why P2 works). [`reports/P11.md`](approaches/cocycle-combine/reports/P11.md).
  - **P10 double-run with defect $x^qy\,z^p$ (✗, certified):** generalizes P0 ($p{=}0$) and P2 ($x{=}z{=}(d)$). The user's two-stage plan works — $\Delta_x$ telescopes the $x$-run to a $q$-free core $C_x(p)=C(x,yz^p)$, $\Delta_z$ the $z$-run to a $p$-free $C_z(q)$ — but each core splits into geometric modes $C_x(p)=\frac1{D(z)}(X^{E_y}C(x,z)X^{pE_z}-N\,Y^{po_z})$, and a $q,p$-free combination needs $C(x,z){=}0$ ∧ $N{=}0$, which forces all three blocks onto one shared fixed point ($C\equiv0$, vacuous). Off it the $Y^{po_z}$ (3-power) growth is unstrippable; **P2 wins only because $x{=}z$ merges the runs**. [`reports/P10.md`](approaches/cocycle-combine/reports/P10.md).
  - **P9 balanced blocks $u_1\cdots u_q$ (✗, certified):** $q$ blocks of equal length and equal gap-sum, arrangement free (generalizes P0's $w^q$). Common diagonal ⇒ rotation sums are **circulant** with geometric generator $c_m=a^{q-1-m}b^m$ ($a{=}Y^o,b{=}X^E$); $c(t)(bt-a)=b^q-a^q$ in $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y][t]/(t^q-1)$ makes $c$ a unit whose top term $b^{q-1}$ forces every nonzero combination to $X$-deg $\ge E(q-1)$ — no $q$-uniform core (P3 obstruction, circulant form). At $q{=}2$ collapses to the P0 antisymmetry core $(a-b)(s_1-s_2)$, ceiling $(2^E+3^o)\mid(s_2-s_1)$, which an escape search up to $(5,9)$ never evades ⇒ forces $s_1=s_2$ (= P0). [`reports/P9.md`](approaches/cocycle-combine/reports/P9.md).
  - **Seed P0 (inherited, proved):** eventually-periodic $w^qw'$ — $S^{0}-S^{|w|}=X^{(q-1)|w|}C(w,w')$, $q$-free ceiling $G\mid C(w,w')$. **P1 circuit $d^o$:** $(X^d-Y)S=D$, core $=G$ (vacuous; Steiner stratum).

### bounded-defect-lever

- **Rationale** — Promoted from the `cocycle-combinatorics` incubator: a cyclic word over a uniform background $d$ whose defects fit one **bounded contiguous arc** $\beta$ (rest a single growing run $d^L$) has the **$o$-independent** exclusion ceiling $G\le\operatorname{sixfree}(S(\beta d)-S(d\beta))$, via shared-frame extraction $S(PMQ)-S(PM'Q)=X^{|P|}Y^{\operatorname{len}Q}(S(M)-S(M'))$ (the dual of inner-core extraction); generalizes structure.md P2/C9 from a single defect to a bounded arc.
- **Status** — active — positive lever **proven + certified** (engine, reach, strongest-exclusion atlas); the $\ge2$-growing-gap no-go boundary is **reduced-but-open** (a cyclotomic-collapse / Lehmer question).
- **Results**
  - **Shared-frame extraction (proved, $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$):** for $\chi(M)=\chi(M')$, the outer frame extracts as the unit $X^{|P|}Y^{\operatorname{len}Q}$, leaving a run-independent local core — the dual of [P1].
  - **Bounded-defect ceiling (proved, one growing run):** $G\le\operatorname{sixfree}(S(\beta d)-S(d\beta))$, independent of $o$; the wedge is a rotation combo (so $\ge G$) yet its 6-free part is bounded. **Corrects** the "$\ge G$" floor framing: exclusion lives on the 6-free axis, not the size axis.
  - **Reach + atlas (proved):** enlarges structure.md P2 (single defect) to bounded arcs; strongest exclusions are single-defect $|e-d|\in\{1,2\}$ (ceiling 1) and genuine 2-defect arcs (ceiling $\ge5$).
  - **No-go boundary (reduced-but-open):** $\ge2$ simultaneously growing gaps ⇒ every rotation-pair difference carries a non-cyclotomic (Mahler $\gt1$) factor of degree $\to\infty$ (Plücker C8 backbone), reducing exclusion to an open cyclotomic-collapse statement; a finite search provably cannot settle it.

### circulant_matrix

- **Rationale** — Child of `cycle-gcd`: the rotation sums are the row sums of an integer matrix $M=\Delta^{-1}C\Delta$ (circulant $C$ with symbol $c_r=2^{rE/o}3^{o-1-r}$, conjugated by the power-of-2 diagonal $\Delta=\operatorname{diag}(2^{e_k-kE/o})$), whose every eigenvalue and whose determinant carry $D=2^E-3^o$ — a candidate a-priori handle on $\gcd_j S^{(j)}$.
- **Status** — active — matrix formula found, derived, and numerically verified; the gcd / Smith-normal-form payoff not yet started.
- **Results**
  - **Factorization (proved):** $\vec S=\Delta^{-1}C\Delta\,\mathbf 1$ with $M_{j,i}=2^{(e_i-e_j)-(i-j)E/o}\,c_{(i-j)\bmod o}\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$; verified to reproduce $S^{(j)}$ on the $1\to1$, $-5\to-7$, and a test $(E{=}8,o{=}5)$ word.
  - **Spectrum + determinant (proved):** eigenvalues $\hat c_\ell=D/(2^{E/o}\omega^\ell-3)$ and $\det M=(-1)^{o+1}D^{o-1}$ (checked: $13^4$ for the $E{=}8,o{=}5$ word).
  - **Number-field rewriting:** $D=\pm N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\beta-3)$ ($\beta=2^{E/o}$), $\det M=N(\hat c_0)$; rotation sums are traces $2^{e_j}S^{(j)}=\operatorname{Tr}(\gamma_W\beta^j\hat c_0)$. **gcd law:** $\gcd_jS^{(j)}=|D|/\operatorname{lcm}_j\delta_j$ ($\delta_j=$ trace-part denominators); each prime $p\mid D$ kept/shed independently, cycle $\iff$ all trace parts integral — a local per-prime target (verified 2005 words).
  - **Circuit case** ([`circuits.md`](approaches/circulant_matrix/circuits.md)): the field machinery never needed $K$ — it lives in the étale algebra $A=\mathbb Q[x]/(x^o-2^E)$ (a field iff $g=\gcd(E,o)=1$, else $A=\prod_iK_i$). All identities transfer verbatim; $D=\prod_{m=0}^{g-1}(2^a-3^b\zeta_g^m)$ factors into $g$ circuits; each prime $p\mid D$ localizes to exactly one circuit field where its keep/shed verdict is decided (verified $(6,4),(12,4),(6,3)$).

### zero-sum-combination

- **Rationale** — Foundational (reusable like `diophantine`): the $\mathbb{Z}$-ideal of $x_1,\dots,x_p$ is $\gcd(x_1,\dots,x_p)\,\mathbb{Z}$ (Bézout); pin the analogue for the **zero-sum** combinations $\{\sum_i\alpha_i x_i:\alpha_i\in\mathbb{Z},\ \sum_i\alpha_i=0\}$ — the regime that kills the common part and isolates differences, an application-agnostic lemma the rotation-sum approaches (`cycle-gcd`/`cocycle-combine`) can cite.
- **Status** — active — foundational answer + right ring $\mathbb{Z}[1/6]$ (a PID) ([`general-theory.md`](approaches/zero-sum-combination/general-theory.md)); iterated construction placed in its classical theory ([`lattice-and-cocycles.md`](approaches/zero-sum-combination/lattice-and-cocycles.md)) and a **full literature review** written ([`LITERATURE-REVIEW.md`](approaches/zero-sum-combination/LITERATURE-REVIEW.md)) — central ideal filtration = augmentation/dimension/N-series theory; cocycle layer = Fox calculus / **Magnus cocycle (Gassner/Burau)**, wedge core = cup product (determinant). Next handle: Gassner/Burau normalization + Passi $Q_n$ bounds vs. the cocycle core.
- **Results**
  - **Answer (proved + sourced, [`general-theory.md`](approaches/zero-sum-combination/general-theory.md)):** $Z(x_1,\dots,x_p)=\langle x_i-x_j\rangle=\gcd_i(x_i-x_1)\,\mathbb{Z}$ — a **principal** ideal generated by the gcd of pairwise differences; minimal generators are the $p-1$ consecutive differences. It is **translation-invariant** (the affine shadow of the gcd), and $\gcd(x_i)=\gcd(x_1,\gcd_i(x_i-x_1))$.
  - **No term "zero-sum ideal" exists**; the object is the **augmentation ideal** (kernel of coefficient-sum $\varepsilon$, generated by differences — [nLab](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/augmentation+ideal)) = image of the **root lattice $A_{p-1}=\{\sum\alpha_i=0\}$** (simple roots $e_k-e_{k+1}$) under evaluation; value-side it is the **ideal of differences / lattice ideal** (Eisenbud–Sturmfels).
  - **Right ring is $\mathbb{Z}[1/6]$, a PID (Euclidean):** localize $\mathbb{Z}$ at $\{2^a3^b\}$; units $\pm2^a3^b$, primes $p\ge5$, Euclidean norm $\varphi=$ the **$6$-free part** — so "stripping powers of $2,3$" $=$ working up to units. $D=2^E-3^o$ is $6$-coprime ⟹ $D\mid S^{(j)}$, $\gcd_jS^{(j)}=|D|$ transfer unchanged. **Principality of the rotation-sum difference ideal restored:** $Z(S^{(j)})=\varphi(\gcd_{i,j}(S^{(i)}-S^{(j)}))\,\mathbb{Z}[1/6]$. (Polynomial companion: Laurent ring $\mathbb{Z}[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}]$, a UFD not a PID.)
  - **Iterated construction $=$ augmentation-ideal filtration, and it captures cocycles** ([`lattice-and-cocycles.md`](approaches/zero-sum-combination/lattice-and-cocycles.md), literature sweep): $L\mapsto\langle\sum\alpha_ix_i:\alpha\in L\rangle$ from sublattices of $\mathbb{Z}^p$ to ideals; the difference step ($L=A_{p-1}$) iterated $=$ powers $I^n$ (Passi). "Central" is literal — dimension subgroups $D_n=G\cap(1+I^n)$, lower central series, $\gamma_c(F)=(\Delta^c{+}1)\cap F$, graded $=$ free Lie algebra (Jennings–Lazard–Quillen). **Cocycles captured exactly** (Fox calculus): $1$-cocycles $=$ derivations $=\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}G}(I,M)$, higher layers $=$ Massey/Zassenhaus. Re-reads `cocycle-combine`: $S$ $=$ off-diagonal $1$-cocycle of the transfer matrix; coboundary $cD(w)=B^1$; wedge core $C(u,v)$ $=$ cup/bracket of two cocycles, Plücker $=$ Jacobi.

### parity-manipulation

- **Rationale** — Engineer perturbations of the parity vector $(b_k)$ so that the 2-adic reconstruction $x_0 = -\sum 2^{b_k}/3^{k+1}$ and a perturbed $x'_0$ differ by a tractable integer, turning parity-pattern questions into explicit Diophantine / divisibility statements.
- **Status** — active — finite-support shifts shown to only re-parametrize the existing Collatz tree; pivoting to infinite-support perturbations.
- **Results**
  - General single-block shift $b'_k = b_k + a\,\mathbf{1}_{k\gt q}$, $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ with $a \gt -c_{q+1}$: $x_0 - x'_0 = -(2^a-1)\,2^{b_{q+1}}\,x_{q+1}/3^{q+1}$. Integer iff $2 \cdot 3^q \mid |a|$. Positive $a$ → predecessor moves; negative $a$ → successor moves (constrained by $|a| \le c_{q+1}-1$).
  - **Key structural no-go:** any finite-support perturbation (single- or multi-block, any signs) leaves $x'_k = x_k$ for $k$ past the support — it only rewrites the past of a fixed downstream node. New arithmetic content can only come from **infinite-support** perturbations (periodic, sub-linear growth, or sparse-unbounded).

### punctured-torus

- **Rationale** — The cycle problem is a divisibility condition on a cocycle over the free group $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ built from the two affine maps $a:x\mapsto\tfrac{3x+1}{2}$, $b:x\mapsto\tfrac{x}{2}$; since $F_2=\pi_1$ of the once-punctured torus, realize $\{a,b\}$ as the affine holonomy/side-pairings of an affine surface $\simeq\Sigma_{1,1}$ so that Syracuse cycles become closed geodesics (with an integrality condition) on a natural affine surface.
- **Status** — active — Stage-1 keystone certified (reducible, cusped, sub-Markoff point). **Stage-2 = Lorentzian (flat)**: $D=2^E-3^o$ = Minkowski interval, criticality = light cone, $S$ = **lightlike** translation. **Spacetime identified in Brunswic [arXiv:2108.12631]** and **fully described**: Syracuse = a point of $T\,\mathrm{Teich}(\Sigma_{1,1})$, a future-complete GH Cauchy-compact flat BTZ spacetime over the once-punctured torus; **property P** — cycle $\iff D\mid S\iff$ its closed spacelike geodesic has an **integer ideal endpoint $S/D$**.
