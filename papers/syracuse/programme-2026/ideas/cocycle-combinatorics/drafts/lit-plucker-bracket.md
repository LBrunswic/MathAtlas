# Leverage R3: the Plücker / bracket algebra — is C8 complete, is $J$ a finer ceiling?

**Promise:** medium. R3 reads the *real* paper on relations among $2\times2$ minors against
STRUCTURE.md §3/§5 and settles the two carried questions with kernel proofs. Two headline outcomes,
one structural correction, one honest null:

1. **C8 is NOT the complete relation of $E_0=I_2$ on our matrix — but for an instructive reason, and
   the generic statement was also half-stated.** Generically (a $2\times n$ matrix with independent
   entries) the first-syzygy module $\operatorname{Syz}(I_2)$ is generated by the **Plücker PAIR** — a
   $D$-row *and* an $S$-row three-term syzygy per triple — with Koszul redundant (Eagon–Northcott /
   Lascoux). STRUCTURE.md only records the $D$-row half (C8); the $S$-row companion
   $\sum_{\rm cyc}S(u)C(v,w)=0$ is an equally genuine generic generator. Our state matrix is **highly
   non-generic** ($2n$ entries are cocycle polynomials in just $X,Y$), and specialization **adds**
   syzygies: $\operatorname{Syz}(\text{cores})\supsetneq\langle\text{Plücker pair},\text{Koszul}\rangle$
   on *both* a distinct-word battery and the rotation/cycle stratum.
2. **On the cycle stratum the extra minimal syzygy is the constant-coefficient telescoping relation
   $\sum_{\rm cyc}C(w_i,w_j)=0$** — and C8$(D)=D\cdot(\text{telescoping})$ is *non-minimal* there. This
   finer relation lives in the difference ideal $J$, linking Q1↔Q2.
3. **$J=\langle S^{(i)}-S^{(j)}\rangle$ is exactly the zero-sum / augmentation ideal** of the rotation
   sums (`approaches/zero-sum-combination/general-theory.md`), principal over $\mathbb{Z}[1/6]$ with
   generator the sixfree gcd of differences. As an ideal it *is* finer than any single difference, but
   its generator is a **multiple of $G=\gcd_jS^{(j)}$** (on a cycle $G=|D|=|2^E-3^o|$): it bottoms out
   exactly on the **rotation-combo floor** and never below — **no finer $o$-independent ceiling**. The
   genuinely small, $o$-independent object (the polynomial gcd $X^a(X-1)$ — the bounded-defect lever's
   monomial core) is a *divisor* of every difference but, $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ being a non-PID, **not a
   member** of $J$, so not a realizable zero-sum core. Honest null on the R3 "finer lever" prize.

## 1. Dictionary (paper ↔ our state matrix)

| Paper (generic $m\times n$ matrix) | Our cocycle layer |
|---|---|
| $m\times n$ matrix of indeterminates $x_{ij}$ | state matrix: **2 rows** $(D,S)$, $n$ columns $\mathbf p(w)=(D(w),S(w))^\top$, words $w$ |
| $2\times2$ minors $\Lambda_2$ | cores $C(u,v)=\det[\mathbf p(u)\,|\,\mathbf p(v)]=E_0=I_2$ (R1) |
| **$m=2$**: $X_{2,n}=\mathrm{Gr}_2(\mathbb C^n)$, **all relations quadratic = Plücker** (p.1) | our $m=2$: relations among cores **as coordinates** = Plücker quadrics |
| $m\ge3$: Plücker insufficient, **cubic** minimal relations (Thm 1.1) | **does NOT apply** (we have $m=2$) |
| **first syzygies $\operatorname{Syz}(I_2)$ of the minors as ring elements** "solved by Lascoux" (§7, p.20) | **C8** $\sum_{\rm cyc}D(u)C(v,w)=0$ — the $D$-row Eagon–Northcott/Lascoux syzygy |
| Rees$(I_2)$ fiber type $\iff\mathcal J$ gen. by relations-of-$A$ **and** $\operatorname{Syz}(I_2)$ (Qn 7.1) | the two layers we must keep apart: *coordinate relations* (Plücker quadrics) vs *element syzygies* (C8) |

**The one mismatch that drives everything.** The paper is about a *generic* matrix; our $2\times n$
matrix has its $2n$ entries valued in $\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ (two variables). It is the
**maximal-minor** case ($t=m=2$), the *easy* classical side of the paper: as coordinates the relations
are just Plücker quadrics, and as ring-element syzygies they are the Eagon–Northcott/Lascoux linear
syzygies. So the paper hands us the **generic answer for free**; the only real question is what our
**specialization** does to it — and that is what the kernel decides.

**Two distinct "relation" notions — do not conflate (the paper separates them in §7).**
- **Coordinate relations** = defining ideal of $X_{2,n}=\mathrm{Gr}_2$: the *quadratic* three-term
  Plücker relations $p_{ij}p_{kl}-p_{ik}p_{jl}+p_{il}p_{jk}=0$ among the cores treated as **independent
  variables**. (Not C8.)
- **Element syzygies** = $\operatorname{Syz}(I_2)\subset R^N$: the *linear* relations
  $\sum g_{ij}\,C_{ij}=0$ with $g\in R$ holding because the cores are **specific polynomials**. This is
  **C8** and its $S$-row companion. Q1 asks about *this* module.

## 2. Q1 — Is C8 the complete generating relation of $E_0=I_2$?

### 2a. The generic truth, in full (corrects STRUCTURE.md §3)
For a generic $2\times n$ matrix, $\operatorname{Syz}(I_2)$ is the first term of the Eagon–Northcott /
Lascoux resolution of the maximal minors. Its minimal generators are the **two** three-term syzygies
per triple of columns:
$$\textstyle\sum_{\rm cyc}D(w_i)\,C(w_j,w_k)=0\quad(\text{$D$-row, $=$ C8}),\qquad
  \sum_{\rm cyc}S(w_i)\,C(w_j,w_k)=0\quad(\text{$S$-row}).$$
**Verified** (`PART B`, control on a genuinely generic $2\times n$, $n=3,4$):
$\operatorname{Syz}=\langle\text{Plücker pair}\rangle$, **Koszul redundant**, and a **single** row's
syzygies are *not enough* — both rows are needed. So STRUCTURE.md's "antisymmetry $+$ C8" omits the
$S$-row generator; the complete generic generator set is the **pair**. (`PART A` checks both rows are
genuine identities.)

### 2b. Transfer to our non-generic matrix: C8 is incomplete
Specialization of a generic matrix can only **add** syzygies. It does:
- **Distinct words, distinct $D$** (e.g. $\{(1),(2),(12),(211)\}$):
  $\operatorname{Syz}(\text{cores})\ne\langle\text{Plücker pair},\text{Koszul}\rangle$ (`PART C`).
- **Cycle / rotation stratum** (all rotations of a word share the rotation-invariant
  $D=X^{|w|}-Y^{\operatorname{len}w}$, so the $D$-row is **constant**): there is an extra **minimal**
  syzygy with **constant coefficients**,
  $$\boxed{\,C(w_j,w_k)-C(w_i,w_k)+C(w_i,w_j)=0\,}\qquad(\text{telescoping / difference relation}),$$
  and $\;$C8$(D)=D\cdot(\text{telescoping})\;$ is now **non-minimal**. Verified
  (`PART C`): the telescoping vector is a genuine syzygy, is **not** in
  $\langle\text{Plücker pair},\text{Koszul}\rangle$, and $\operatorname{Syz}$ is strictly larger.

**Why (mechanism).** On rotations $C(w_i,w_j)=D\cdot(S^{(j)}-S^{(i)})=D\,\delta_{ij}$, and since $D$ is a
non-zero-divisor $\operatorname{Syz}(\text{cores})=\operatorname{Syz}(\{\delta_{ij}\})$. The differences
are linearly dependent, $\delta_{ij}=\delta_{0j}-\delta_{0i}$, which is the constant-coefficient
telescoping; C8 is just $D$ times it. **The specialization drops the syzygy degree from $\deg D$
($\sim|w|$) to $0$.** That degree drop is the precise content of "C8 is non-minimal / incomplete on the
cycle stratum," and it is exactly the difference relation of $J$ — Q1 feeds Q2.

> **Verdict Q1.** *Generically* $\operatorname{Syz}(I_2)$ for a $2\times n$ matrix is generated by the
> Plücker **pair** (Lascoux/Eagon–Northcott), Koszul redundant — STRUCTURE.md's lone C8 is half of it.
> *On our matrix* C8 is **not complete**: specialization adds syzygies, and on the Collatz-relevant
> cycle stratum the new minimal generator is the constant-coefficient telescoping/difference relation
> (C8 $=D\times$ it). No *cubic* coordinate relations enter (those are the paper's $m\ge3$ phenomenon;
> we are $m=2$, pure Plücker).

## 3. Q2 — Is the difference ideal $J$ a finer, $o$-independent invariant?

### 3a. $J$ = the zero-sum / augmentation ideal (confirmed)
$J=\langle S^{(i)}-S^{(j)}\rangle$ is verbatim the zero-sum ideal
$Z(S^{(0)},\dots,S^{(o-1)})$ of `general-theory.md`: the image of the augmentation ideal / root lattice
$A_{o-1}$ under evaluation, with $G=\gcd_jS^{(j)}=\gcd\!\big(S^{(0)},\ \gcd_{ij}(S^{(i)}-S^{(j)})\big)$.
**Verified** (`PART D`, battery): the split $G=\gcd(S^{(0)},\Delta)$ holds, $\Delta:=\gcd_{ij}$diff.
Over $\mathbb{Z}[1/6]$ (a PID) $J$ is **principal**, generator $\varphi(\Delta)$ = sixfree gcd of
differences (general-theory.md). On a cycle $E_0|_{\rm rot}=\langle D\rangle\cdot J$ (STRUCTURE.md §3).

### 3b. The collapse: $J$'s generator bottoms out at the floor $G$
- **$J$ *is* finer than a single difference** (an ideal beats an element): $\Delta\mid$ each difference
  and $\Delta\lt\min_{ij}|S^{(i)}-S^{(j)}|$ in every test (`PART D`). The "pairwise vs all" question is
  moot — all-differences gcd $=$ spanning-tree gcd $\lt$ a single difference.
- **But it never goes below $G$.** Each difference is divisible by $G$, so $G\mid\Delta$, hence
  $\Delta\ge G$ and even $\operatorname{sixfree}(\Delta)$ is a multiple of $G$. Verified (`PART D`).
  On a genuine cycle $G=|D|=|2^E-3^o|$, so the *best* ceiling $J$ can give is
  $|2^E-3^o|\le\Delta$ — **tautological** (since $\Delta\ge|D|$). This is precisely the
  **rotation-combination floor** (`drafts/rotation-combo-floor.md`): every nonzero zero-sum combination
  of full rotation sums is $\ge G$. $J$ is the *ideal-theoretic name* for that floor, and its generator
  is the smallest floor element — which equals $G$, never less.

### 3c. The finer object exists but is non-realizable (non-PID obstruction)
The **polynomial** gcd of the differences is small and $o$-independent — e.g.
$\gcd_{\mathbb{Q}[X,Y]}(\{S^{(i)}-S^{(j)}\})=X(X-1)$ for single-defect/primitive families (more
generally $X^a(X-1)$), which is *exactly* the bounded-defect lever's monomial core
(`bounded-defect-lever`, `rotation-combo-floor`). It **divides every difference**. But
$\mathbb{Z}[X,Y]$ (and $\mathbb{Q}[X,Y]$) is **not a PID**, and that gcd is **not a member** of
$J=\langle\text{diffs}\rangle$ — verified by an agca containment test (`PART E`, `False` on all
batteries). So it cannot be realized as a single zero-sum combination/core: you cannot "divide out the
gcd." The lever instead picks a **specific small member** of $J$ — a sub-block difference
$C(u,v)=S(uv)-S(vu)=S^{(i)}-S^{(j)}$ with $|D(u)|$ small — whose sixfree part is bounded; that member is
$\ge G$ (on the floor) yet $o$-independent on the **sixfree axis**, which is where exclusion lives.

> **Verdict Q2.** $J$ = the zero-sum/augmentation ideal (confirmed); principal over $\mathbb{Z}[1/6]$,
> generator $=$ sixfree gcd of differences. It is finer than a single difference but **collapses to the
> rotation-combo floor**: $\operatorname{gen}(J)$ is a multiple of $G=|D|$, never below, so it gives **no
> finer $o$-independent ceiling**. $J$ is the correct *home* of the bounded-defect lever (its winning
> cores are members), but the ideal/gcd structure does **not** beat the specific members already used —
> the genuinely small polynomial gcd $X^a(X-1)$ is a divisor, not a member (non-PID). **Honest null on
> the prize.**

## 4. What is genuinely new (vs WHAT WE KNOW)

- **(structural, new)** STRUCTURE.md §3 should record that the *complete* generic Plücker syzygy module
  is the **pair** ($D$-row C8 **and** $S$-row $\sum_{\rm cyc}S(u)C(v,w)=0$), with Koszul redundant —
  the Lascoux/Eagon–Northcott first syzygies of the $2\times n$ maximal minors. The note had only C8.
- **(structural, new)** On our non-generic matrix **C8 is incomplete**; on the cycle stratum the new
  *minimal* relation is the constant-coefficient telescoping $\sum_{\rm cyc}C=0$ (C8 $=D\times$ it,
  hence non-minimal). This is a degree-drop of the syzygy from $\deg D$ to $0$ — a concrete sense in
  which the cores carry *more* relations than the generic Grassmannian, despite the Fitting filtration
  terminating (R1).
- **(consolidation, not a new lever)** $J$ = zero-sum/augmentation ideal $=$ the ideal-theoretic name of
  the rotation-combo floor; its generator collapses to $G=|D|$. The finer polynomial gcd ($X^a(X-1)$, the
  lever's monomial core) is non-realizable as a member (non-PID). So Q2's "finer ceiling" is a **null**,
  but it *unifies* three previously separate notes (`rotation-combo-floor`, `bounded-defect-lever`,
  `zero-sum-combination`) under one object.

Not re-confirmed (already known, R1): cores $=E_0=I_2=$ top Fitting ideal; cores are the $2\times2$
minors / cup product; $S$ injective per stratum.
