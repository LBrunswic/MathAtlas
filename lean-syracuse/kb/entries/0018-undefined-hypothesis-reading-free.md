title: Refute a statement with an undefined hypothesis without choosing its reading
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-21
---
Found by T-syr-upper-bounds (« intertwined », `cor:intertwined_i_`). Take the reading I as a parameter, with the hypothesis that the witness satisfies I, and refute the printed conclusion for every such I (`intertwined_printed_false`). The refutation then covers every reading that admits the witness. If the conclusion holds without the hypothesis, prove the true form with the hypothesis dropped (`intertwined_alt`, `intertwined_aun`). Neither needs a ruling on what the word means.
