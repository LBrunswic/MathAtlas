Found by T-syr-completion. `Syracuse.toZ2` (`Syracuse.Completion`) is `PadicInt.lift` of the maps to `ZMod (2ⁿ)`; A/2ⁿA ≃ ℤ/2ⁿℤ comes from `CharP` and "every x is an integer mod 2ⁿ" (`exists_int_two_pow_dvd_sub`); a ring hom A → `ZMod (2ⁿ)` is unique (`ringHom_zmodPow_ext`), which gives the uniqueness of `toZ2`.
