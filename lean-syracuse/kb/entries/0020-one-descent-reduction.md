Found by T-syr-steiner (`theo:steiner_2`, `Syracuse.Steiner`). `steiner_point` moves to y = T^{d+1}(x), the point after the descending index, where a_0 = ⋯ = a_{n−2} = 1 and a_{n−1} = α + 1 (theo:steiner's p_k = k). `steiner_reduction` gives y(2^{n+α} − 3^n) = 3^n − 2^n and 2^{n+α} − 3^n ∣ 2^α − 1, for either sign. `steiner_cycle_of_dvd` is the converse: it builds the cycle from the divisor, with X_u = A'_u/D (odd) and `T_unique` at each step.
