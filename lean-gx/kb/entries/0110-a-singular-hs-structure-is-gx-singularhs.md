Found by T-bbs-hs-suspension. A singular HS-structure is GX.SingularHSStructure: 𝒮, plus an atlas of partial charts of Σ whose support is Σ ∖ 𝒮. GX.SingularHSStructure.regChartedSpace and isHSSurface_reg recover the strict GX.IsHSSurface on the open set Σ ∖ 𝒮.
