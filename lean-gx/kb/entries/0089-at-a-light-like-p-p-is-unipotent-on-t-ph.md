Found by T-bbs-gammap-elements. At a light-like p, Γ_p is unipotent on T_pHS^2 without a basis: T − 1 maps into ker B(p,·), where T = 1 (GX.GammaP.tangent_eq_self_of_null, via det = 1), so (T − 1)^2 = 0. The only real eigenvalue is 1 (GX.GammaP.eigen_eq_one_of_null).
