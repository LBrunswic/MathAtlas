Found by T-bbs-psl2-normal-forms. Conjugacy in SL(2,R) is decided by GX.sl_exists_conj_of_trace_eq: same trace, and the forms slQuad A = det[v | A v] = c x^2 + (d-a) x y - b y^2 both take a positive value. [v | A v] with q(v) = 1 conjugates A to slCompanion (tr A). For the other sign use A^-1 in SL (slQuad_inv) or -A in PSL (slQuad_neg, pslMk_neg).
