---
id: 0010
title: Detect an infinitesimal non-null loop with an invariant valued in a quotient group
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-20
---
A loop homotopic into every neighbourhood of a point but not null-homotopic (Griffiths-type
spaces) is invisible to every continuous invariant valued in a homotopically Hausdorff target.
Detect it with a homotopy invariant valued in a quotient group, e.g. the linking matrix modulo
finite rank (`GX.grifTheta`), built from exp-lifts and winding numbers (`GX.cWinding`)
(T-gx-slsc-compact).
