To lift an orientation-reversing map of ℓ(p), e.g. GX.linkReflect B = ℓ(−p)⁻¹ ∘ B, to ℝP̃¹: θ_B = tangentNeg θ ∘ [B] (GX.tangentMove) is oriented, linkMove B ∘ linkCover θ_B = linkCover (tangentNeg θ) (GX.linkMove_linkCover), and exists_rayLift with A = 1 between θ and θ_B gives F ∈ G̃ with R̂(linkCover θ t) = linkCover θ (−F t) (GX.exists_linkReflect_linkCover).
