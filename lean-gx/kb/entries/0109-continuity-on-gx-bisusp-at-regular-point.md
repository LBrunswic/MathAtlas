Found by T-bbs-hs-suspension. On GX.BiSusp, continuity at a regular point reduces to continuity of the composite with mk' (GX.BiSusp.nhds_mk: 𝓝 (mk' q) = map mk' (𝓝 q)). Continuity at the apices comes free from the quotient topology (GX.BiSusp.continuous_lift).
