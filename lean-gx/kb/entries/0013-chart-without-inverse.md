Found by T-gx-troyanov-triangle. `GX.isOpenMap_restrict_of_isCompact`: f injective on an open V, continuous on a compact K ⊇ V, f(V) open and f(K ∖ V) disjoint from f(V) make f an open map on V; `ofContinuousOpenRestrict` then gives the `OpenPartialHomeomorph`.
