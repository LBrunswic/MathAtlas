Found by T-btz-c1-spacelike-development. Causal.Flat.SpacelikeDevelopment F is a no-boundary notion: on a space with a boundary point it fails. For the btz strip, SpacelikeDevelopment (graphDev g R) <-> R <= 0 for every g (GX.E0Surgery.spacelikeDevelopment_graphDev_iff); strict lem_surface_complete_two_lip is vacuous. Use SpacelikeDevelopment (graphDevOn g Omega) with an open Omega containing the strip, or UnifSpacelike g R (GXScaffold.BTZ.C1SpacelikeDevLip).
