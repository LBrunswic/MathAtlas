Found by T-bbs-rp1-circles. Every lift of g in SL(2,R) to R commutes with t -> t + 1. The degree is a homomorphism to {1, -1}, trivial on squares, and SL(2,R) is a product of unipotents (GX.slDeg_eq_one, GX.sl_eq_upper_lower_upper). No connectedness of SL(2,R) is needed.
