Found by T-btz-reg-complete-length. On the rim r = R of a spear the printed E0 metric -2dtau dr + dr^2 + r^2 dtheta^2 is R^2 dtheta^2, whatever the surface: rim arcs have length <= pi R. Thinning spears to summable radii (LFSurgeryData.exists_summable, from posSumOfEncodable) makes errors across infinitely many spears additive and summable.
