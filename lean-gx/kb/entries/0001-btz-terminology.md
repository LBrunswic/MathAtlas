gx's BTZ model (`GX.btzDev`, the parabolic holonomy, a null `u`; `E₀`, the singular line lightlike) is
what Barbot–Bonsante–Schlenker (*Collisions of particles I*, arXiv:1010.3602) call an **extreme
BTZ-like** singularity. Their plain **BTZ-like** singularity is spacelike with hyperbolic holonomy:
in gx that is the `misner_btz` boost family of `GX.Singular.Quotient`. Read any BBS citation with
this dictionary (papers/bbs/REVIEW.md §F).
