title: A glued van Kampen invariant can be glued again: one gluing per bad point
kind: pattern
created: 2026-11-20
---
`GX.VanKampen.Data.Φ` is multiplicative (`GX.VanKampen.Data.Φ_trans`), so a glued invariant can be
glued again: one gluing per bad point, the base point moved by conjugation along a tether
(`GX.grifD1`, `GX.grifD2`); `GX.grifMkData` builds the data from three path-connected opens and an
invariant on U that is trivial on U ∩ V (`GX.Topology.SLSCCompact`, T-gx-slsc-compact).
