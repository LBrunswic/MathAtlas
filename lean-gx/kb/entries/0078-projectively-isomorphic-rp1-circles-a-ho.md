Projectively isomorphic RP1-circles (a homeomorphism that is an IsGXMorphism PSL(2,R) RP1) have the same lifted holonomies and real angles: GX.hasLiftedHolonomy_iff_of_isGXMorphism, GX.hasRealAngle_iff_of_isGXMorphism. GX.exists_projIso_of_lift builds the isomorphism from a map phi of universal covers with phi(s+1) = phi s + 1 and D2 o phi = D1. Its surjectivity comes from mathlib's CircleDeg1Lift.continuous_iff_surjective.
