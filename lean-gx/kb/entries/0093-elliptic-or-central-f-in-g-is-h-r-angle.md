Found by T-bbs-psl2-normal-forms. In G~, an elliptic or central f is h R~(angle f) h^-1 (GX.GTilde.isRotLift_angle). A conjugacy in PSL(2,R) lifts to G~ up to delta^n (GX.GTilde.exists_delta_zpow_of_toPSL_conj), exactly when both elements have fixed points (GX.GTilde.exists_conj_of_toPSL_conj).
