## Round-1 conclusions after round 2

- **Confirmed (strengthened):**
  - Hausdorffness of the gluing is the core of maximal-development existence: Chruściel's Lemma 3.3 is a third independent instance.
  - Rigidity and a size bound are separate, essential inputs (Chruściel Thm 2.2).
  - "Locating an extension" is purely causal (re-proved in two synthetic frameworks).
  - J-based definitions are needed below Lipschitz regularity.
  - The singular set must be kept as points (conical well-posedness and Cauchy slices exist only with the axis).
- **Qualified:**
  - "Causal once I± is open" holds for piecewise-C¹ timelike curves (Sbierski's convention). With Lipschitz curves it is a genuine hypothesis.
  - The two-cone mechanism reaches modified gravity only with the nesting assumed.
- **Gap narrowed but open:**
  - Conical lines appear in the literature only as static cones (PDE and Colombeau) or as generalized cones (LLS, by inference).
  - BTZ lines are excluded by every framework read: synthetic, distributional and Colombeau. ETT is the only framework that holds them.
