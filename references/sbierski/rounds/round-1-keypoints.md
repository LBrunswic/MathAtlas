
## Key points
- [T1 chrusciel2012lorentzian] [V] Motivation: the differentiability needed for causality arguments constrains the Choquet-Bruhat–Geroch theorem (§1).
- [T1 chrusciel2012lorentzian] [V] For metrics that are merely continuous, light-cones need not be hypersurfaces: there are light-cones with non-empty interior (§1, Example 1.11).

- [T1 galloway2017timelike] [P] Core of Thm 3.3: in a near-Minkowskian chart around p ∈ ∂⁺M, a small set V (diamond of the wide metric η^(1/2)) is globally hyperbolic for g_ext; a maximizer α from q=γ(0) to p splits into σ ⊂ M and λ; σ is a geodesic; if it were null, then L(α)=L(λ)<L(γ), contradicting maximality, for ε small (§3).

## Contradictions and tensions
- [T1 chrusciel2012lorentzian] [[minguzzi2019causality]]: Minguzzi presents C&G's theory as "seemed to work well only under locally Lipschitz regularity and did not include results involving lightlike geodesics", and shows that by using causal-curve definitions and local achronality the theory extends to upper semi-continuous cones, with Cauchy horizons generated by lightlike geodesics even in bubbling cases. A refinement rather than a contradiction.
- [T1 chrusciel2012lorentzian] [[kunzinger2018lorentzian]] and [[galloway2017timelike]]: both depend on C&G's sandwich approximation (Prop. 1.2) for C^0 results; consistent.
- [T1 galloway2017timelike] [[sbierski2015c]]: that paper proves C^0-inextendibility of the Schwarzschild exterior via timelike diameter + future one-connectedness; this paper makes the one-connectedness unnecessary in the complete region. Not a contradiction, a replacement of method. Mirrored in [[sbierski2017proof]].
- [T1 kunzinger2018lorentzian] [[galloway2017timelike]]: consistent — both note that τ (Lorentzian distance) fails lower semicontinuity for C^0 metrics; KS need causal plainness to recover it.
- [T1 kunzinger2018lorentzian] [[minguzzi2019causality]]: KS rely on local Lipschitz continuity of the cone field (Th. 5.16), whereas Minguzzi's causality theory works for merely upper semi-continuous cone fields; KS flag the gap as an open question.
- [T1 minguzzi2019causality] [[chrusciel2012lorentzian]]: C&G found that C^0 causality "seemed to work well only under locally Lipschitz regularity"; Minguzzi shows the causal ladder, domains of dependence and Cauchy horizons survive under mere upper semicontinuity, provided one changes definitions (causal-curve based, local achronality for lightlike geodesics). Not a contradiction of results — a change of definitions — but the two disagree on where the theory "breaks".
- [T1 minguzzi2019causality] [[samann2015global]]: Sämann gets GH ⇒ Cauchy hypersurface by stability + the *smooth* theory; Minguzzi proves it intrinsically for closed cone structures (Th. 2.42 via Geroch's volume function on a wider cone structure). Consistent conclusions, different dependence on smooth results.
- [T1 minguzzi2019causality] [[galloway2017timelike]]: consistent — lower semicontinuity of the time separation fails at C^0; Minguzzi handles length only via Lorentz–Finsler cone structures with F(∂C)=0 under local Lipschitz hypotheses (as used in [[kunzinger2018lorentzian]] Prop. 5.14).
- [T1 samann2015global] [[minguzzi2019causality]]: same equivalences, but Minguzzi proves GH ⇒ Cauchy time function intrinsically (Geroch volume function on a wider cone structure) for closed cone structures, whereas Sämann defers to the smooth theory. Consistent results.
- [T1 samann2015global] [[galloway2017timelike]]: reproves Sämann's Prop. 6.4 (maximisers) and notes only compact diamonds are needed; consistent.
- [T1 sbierski2013existence] [[eperon2018predictability]] §4.2: the §1.4.1 quasilinear sketch here "tacitly assumes" U1 ∩ U2 is connected; false in general (their Born–Infeld example). Not a contradiction of the Einstein theorem, but the "union of all GHDs" analogy fails for fixed-background equations without extra hypotheses.
- [T1 sbierski2013existence] [[graf2023uniqueness]] re-uses the gluing pattern; their obstruction to Hausdorffness ("intertwined timelike geodesics") plays the role that corresponding boundary points play here, but here the obstruction is *excluded by the PDE* (local uniqueness), whereas there it must be *assumed away*.
