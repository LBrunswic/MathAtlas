## Summary
Minguzzi develops the whole of classical causality theory for *closed cone structures*: on a C^1 (hence smoothable) manifold, an upper semi-continuous field x ↦ C_x of closed, sharp, convex, non-empty cones in T_xM \ 0 (equivalently a closed subbundle of the slit tangent bundle); causal curves are locally absolutely continuous solutions of the differential inclusion ẋ ∈ C_x a.e. Non-round cones and no regularity beyond upper semicontinuity are allowed. He proves the full causal ladder, limit-curve theorems, the Seifert relation J_S (intersection of causal relations of all wider C^0 proper cone structures) is a closed preorder, the equivalence stable causality ⇔ antisymmetry of J_S ⇔ K-causality ⇔ existence of a time function ⇔ existence of a smooth temporal function (Th. 2.30), the equivalence of four definitions of global hyperbolicity and their stability under opening cones (Th. 2.39), and GH ⇔ Cauchy time function ⇔ smooth steep Cauchy temporal function ⇔ (stable) Cauchy hypersurface, with smooth splitting (Th. 2.45). For *proper* cone structures (non-empty interior) he gets domains of dependence and Cauchy horizons generated by lightlike geodesics, the latter defined by local achronality. Metric notions are handled by a "product trick": a Lorentz–Finsler function F is encoded as a cone structure on M × R. Applications: Lorentzian manifolds embeddable in Minkowski space are exactly the "stable" spacetimes; the Connes-type distance formula.

## Relevance to the questions
- **(i) smooth/metric vs purely causal or order-topological.** Manifold structure is used everywhere: M is a C^r manifold (connected, Hausdorff, second countable), cones live in tangent spaces, causal curves are a.e.-differentiable solutions of a differential inclusion, and *stability* (Seifert relation J_S, stable causality, stable Cauchy hypersurfaces, Th. 2.39) is defined by opening the cones to wider locally Lipschitz proper cone fields — this needs the tangent bundle. Smoothing of anti-Lipschitz time functions (§3.5) is analytic. Purely order-topological: K = smallest closed preorder containing J and K-causality (Def. 2.14); Auslander–Levin (Th. 3.13: closed preorder on a second countable locally compact Hausdorff space has a continuous utility, and utilities represent the preorder); Th. 2.37 (closed relation ⇒ R^±(K) closed for compact K); Th. 2.38 (compact causal emeralds ⇒ J closed), whose proof uses only compactness and the finite intersection property; Prop. 2.19 in Nachbin's language of T1/T2-preordered spaces; and the remark that causal simplicity and global hyperbolicity belong to "topological preordered spaces" (closed antisymmetric preorder + compact causally convex hulls of compacta).
- **(ii) where ETT sits.** ETT (a topological space with a causality, no tangent bundle) is *more general in the base* (no manifold, no cones: singular (G,X)-manifolds with conical and BTZ lines are admissible) but *less equipped* than closed cone structures: it has no notion of "wider causality", hence no Seifert relation, no stable causality, no stability of global hyperbolicity, and no smoothing. ETT's own text says stable causality "is not yet clearly defined for topological spacetimes" (ETT.tex §admissible measures). Minguzzi's K relation is the natural ETT replacement for J_S: it is defined from J and the topology alone, and Th. 2.30 shows K = J_S and K-causality ⇔ existence of a time function for closed cone structures. ETT's time-function route (Dieckmann admissible measures + Levin for closed preorders) is the same volume-function/Auslander–Levin route Minguzzi uses. Conical singular lines E_α and BTZ lines E_0 are *not* closed cone structures as stated (no tangent cone at the singular line in the given smooth structure); whether some smooth structure makes E_α (α<2π) an upper semi-continuous cone field — by convexifying the limit cones on the singular line — is an open question raised here, not a claim of the paper. A "PDE with several characteristic cones" fits Minguzzi only via a single convex cone per point (e.g. the convex hull of the characteristic cones); non-round cones are exactly what the framework was built for (§1).
- **(iii) Lean candidates for lean-causal.** (a) `closed_of_compact_emeralds`: in a Hausdorff topological space with a preorder J such that J⁺(K₁) ∩ J⁻(K₂) is compact for all compact K₁, K₂, J is closed in M × M (Th. 2.38, proof purely topological). (b) `closedRel_image_compact_closed`: a closed relation R has R^±(K) closed for compact K (Th. 2.37). (c) the K-relation: define K as the smallest closed preorder containing J in ETT, and prove "K antisymmetric ⇒ existence of a time function" from Auslander–Levin/Levin (ETT already has Levin for closed preorders). (d) Def. 2.20 (γ): "causally simple + compact causally convex hulls of compacta" as an ETT definition of global hyperbolicity and its equivalence with ETT's own. These are the stepping stones Minguzzi himself marks as belonging to topological preordered spaces.

## Key points
- [P] Causal ladder holds in full for closed cone structures (§1, Th. 2.47; the sentence spans a page break in the text so it is not quoted).
- [V] Stable causality ⇔ antisymmetry of J_S ⇔ K-causality ⇔ empty stable recurrent set ⇔ existence of a time function ⇔ existence of a smooth temporal function; then J_S = K (Th. 2.30, proved §3.2–3.6).
- [V] K is defined purely order-topologically as the smallest closed preorder containing J (Def. 2.14).
- [V] Auslander–Levin: closed preorder on second countable locally compact Hausdorff space ⇒ continuous utility, and utilities recover the preorder (Th. 3.13).
- [V] Time functions are "a byproduct of the Seifert relation J_S being closed", not of Lorentzian cones (§1).
- [P] Four equivalent definitions of global hyperbolicity (α non-imprisonment + bounded causally convex hulls; β causality + compact emeralds; γ causal simplicity + compact hulls; δ stable causality + compact Seifert diamonds) (Def. 2.20, Th. 2.38–2.39).
- [V] Stability of global hyperbolicity: GH closed cone structure has J_S = J and a GH locally Lipschitz proper cone structure with wider cones (Th. 2.39).
- [P] GH ⇔ Cauchy time function ⇔ smooth h-steep Cauchy temporal function ⇔ (stable) Cauchy hypersurface; M smoothly ≅ R × S (Th. 2.45); proof via Geroch's volume function on a *wider* cone structure (Th. 2.42).
- [V] Compact causal emeralds ⇒ J closed, by a pure compactness argument (Th. 2.38).
- [V] Causal simplicity and global hyperbolicity can be regarded as notions of topological preordered spaces (§2.10, citing [83]).
- [V] I∘J ∪ J∘I ⊂ I (push-up) fails for proper cone structures, yet most results survive (§1); Th. 2.8: for proper cone structures, push-up ⇔ no causal bubbling.
- [P] Cauchy horizons of proper cone structures are generated by lightlike geodesics defined by local achronality, answering Chruściel–Grant's question on bubbling (Th. 2.32; [[chrusciel2012lorentzian]]).
- [P] Metric theory = causality on M × R ("product trick"); causal versions of Penrose, Hawking, Hawking–Penrose singularity theorems for anisotropic cones under weak regularity (§2.13–2.15).
- [V] Causal curves are locally absolutely continuous solutions of the differential inclusion ẋ ∈ C_x a.e. (§2.1).
- [P] Cites Sbierski's C^0-inextendibility of Schwarzschild [22] and Galloway–Ling–Sbierski [23] as applications of C^0 causality that left geodesic questions open (§1).

## Methods and evidence
- Data / setting: pure mathematics; closed (upper semi-continuous) and proper cone structures on C^1 manifolds; Lorentz–Finsler spaces via cone structures on M × R.
- Baselines: classical C^2 Lorentzian causality (Hawking–Ellis, Minguzzi–Sánchez), C^0 work of Fathi–Siconolfi, Bernard–Suhr, Chruściel–Grant, Sämann.
- Evaluation (metrics, seeds, variance, held-out?): full proofs; self-contained ("we do not use any advanced result from mathematical relativity").
- Artifacts (code / data / weights): none (not applicable).

## Limitations and caveats
- Requires a manifold and convex cones in tangent spaces: no non-manifold points, no non-convex cone (e.g. a union of two disjoint characteristic cones), no degenerate (empty-interior) cone at a singular line in proper-cone results.
- Results on domains of dependence and Cauchy horizons need *proper* cone structures (non-empty interior), not just closed ones.
- Stability notions (J_S, stable Cauchy hypersurface) are defined by enlarging cones in the tangent bundle; they have no meaning without that bundle.
- Not addressed: Einstein equations, matter, or any (G,X)-structure / holonomy; no singular lines.

## Contradictions and tensions
- [[chrusciel2012lorentzian]]: C&G found that C^0 causality "seemed to work well only under locally Lipschitz regularity"; Minguzzi shows the causal ladder, domains of dependence and Cauchy horizons survive under mere upper semicontinuity, provided one changes definitions (causal-curve based, local achronality for lightlike geodesics). Not a contradiction of results — a change of definitions — but the two disagree on where the theory "breaks".
- [[samann2015global]]: Sämann gets GH ⇒ Cauchy hypersurface by stability + the *smooth* theory; Minguzzi proves it intrinsically for closed cone structures (Th. 2.42 via Geroch's volume function on a wider cone structure). Consistent conclusions, different dependence on smooth results.
- [[galloway2017timelike]]: consistent — lower semicontinuity of the time separation fails at C^0; Minguzzi handles length only via Lorentz–Finsler cone structures with F(∂C)=0 under local Lipschitz hypotheses (as used in [[kunzinger2018lorentzian]] Prop. 5.14).

## Open questions
- Does every singular flat spacetime with conical lines E_α (α < 2π) admit a smooth structure in which its causality is that of a closed cone structure? (If yes, all of Minguzzi's theory applies off-the-shelf to massive particles; BTZ lines E_0, where the singular line is lightlike, look harder.)
- Is ETT's K± (continuously reachable future) related to Minguzzi/Sorkin–Woolgar's K relation, e.g. is the closure-of-transitive-closure of ETT's J equal to K, and does K-causality ⇒ time function hold in ETT via Levin?
- Can "stability" be defined in ETT via a family of coarser causalities (a topology on causalities), so that stable causality and the Seifert relation have ETT analogues?
