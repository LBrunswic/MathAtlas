## Summary
The paper asks whether an extendible spacetime has a unique *maximal* future boundary. Instead of spacetime extensions (which cannot be unique beyond the boundary, Remark 10), it uses "regular future g-boundary extensions": topological manifolds with boundary N containing M as interior, every boundary point being the future endpoint of a timelike geodesic, with topology near the boundary generated by "timelike thickenings" of geodesic bundles (Defs 7, 9). Ordered families are glued by disjoint union and quotient, giving maximal elements by Zorn (Thm 1). For two arbitrary extensions the glued quotient is Hausdorff provided M contains no "intertwined" timelike geodesics (geodesics that approach each other arbitrarily closely without eventually staying close; Def 34, Lemma 44), which yields a unique maximal regular future g-boundary extension for strongly causal C^2 spacetimes (Thm 2 / Thm 46). Remark 47 notes this second result is dezornifiable exactly as in [[sbierski2013existence]].

## Key points
- [V] Main theorem: a strongly causal C^2 spacetime which is regular future g-boundary extendible and has no intertwined future-directed timelike geodesics has a unique maximal regular future g-boundary extension (Thm 2, §1; Thm 46, §5).
- [P] Uniqueness is only topological (up to homeomorphism of manifolds with boundary); no metric is extended to the boundary and none is used in the proofs (§1, p. 3).
- [V] Thm 1: for any partially ordered set of equivalence classes of regular future g-boundary extensions a maximal element exists (Zorn) (§1, Cor 33).
- [P] Obstruction: intertwined timelike geodesics (as in Taub–NUT / Misner) can make the glued quotient non-Hausdorff, giving inequivalent maximal extensions (Def 34, Lemma 44, §1 p. 4).
- [V] Dezornification: the proof of Thm 46 does not need Zorn and is "very similar" to Sbierski's Thms 2.7/2.8; identifying limit points of the same timelike geodesic plays the role of identifying points of the common GHD (Remark 47 and its footnotes 5–6).
- [P] Compatibility: for globally hyperbolic M and a C^2 extension with empty past boundary, ι(M) ∪ ∂^+ι(M) is a regular future g-boundary extension (§3); below C^{1,1} this is "quite doubtful", and Sbierski's lemma that every point of ∂^+ is reached by a timelike geodesic seems to need more than C^1 (§1 item (iii), §6).
- [P] Iff-version: a maximal extension exists iff no intertwined pair acquires endpoints in two regular extensions; whether this already forbids all intertwined geodesics is open (Remark 47, end).
- [V] Context: Sbierski proved local uniqueness of C^{0,1}_loc extensions anchored on a common timelike curve, and gave examples where it fails below C^{0,1}_loc (§1, p. 2–3).

## Methods and evidence
- Data / setting: C^2 strongly causal spacetimes; extensions as topological manifolds with boundary; topology encoded through geodesic thickenings in TM with an auxiliary Riemannian metric.
- Baselines: Chruściel's maximal conformal boundary extensions (null thickenings), Geroch's g-boundary, Galloway–Ling–Sbierski future/past boundary results, Sbierski's local uniqueness.
- Evaluation: rigorous proofs; no new examples of regular future g-boundary extendibility are produced.
- Artifacts: none.

## Limitations and caveats
- Authors concede: only regular future g-boundary extensions, not spacetime extensions; substantial "hidden regularity" in Def 9; no criterion for when such extensions exist (§1, §6).
- Uniqueness is topological; the metric on the boundary is not addressed.
- The statement of Thm 46 in the text drops the word "unique" relative to Thm 2 (typo; the proof does establish uniqueness of the set-theoretic maximal element).
- Not a Sbierski paper; it is in the KB as a citing work that extends his local uniqueness and dezornification.
- Abstract matches the theorems (`claims_exceed_evidence=false`).

## Relevance to the questions
**(i) Smooth vs. order-topological.** The final object and the uniqueness are topological; the *metric* enters through (a) timelike geodesics and their continuous dependence on initial data (thickenings), (b) the C^2 hypothesis, (c) strong causality. The gluing step (disjoint union, quotient, openness of the quotient map, Hausdorffness) is pure topology, exactly as in [[sbierski2013existence]] §3.3.
**(ii) What ETT could state or generalise.** The pair "glue along the common part / Hausdorff iff no bad boundary pairs" now appears in two places — CBG (bad pairs = corresponding boundary points, excluded by local uniqueness) and boundary extensions (bad pairs = endpoints of intertwined geodesics, excluded by hypothesis). ETT could replace timelike geodesics by order-theoretic timelike curves and thickenings by I^±-type neighbourhoods; whether "regular" extensions can be characterised purely causally is the real question, and the authors' §6 discussion of choosing the topology (g-, b-, c-boundaries) suggests a causal-boundary (GKP) route that ETT is well placed for. For singular flat spacetimes, the future boundary at a conical or BTZ line is a natural test: intertwined geodesics plausibly occur near BTZ-type lines (cf. Misner, a quotient of Minkowski by a boost — the same holonomy type as BTZ), which would make the maximal boundary non-unique there.
**(iii) Candidate statements for lean-causal.** (a) A general *family-gluing lemma*: for a family of spaces N_α with open embeddings of a common M with dense image, the quotient (⊔ N_α)/∼ (identify points that are limits of the same curve/filter of M) is Hausdorff iff pairwise Hausdorff — shared with CBG's Thm 2.8 item 1. (b) An ETT-level definition of "intertwined" timelike curves (pairs that are neither eventually-close nor eventually-separated) and a Misner/BTZ example. Neither is as near-term as the CBG lemmas in [[sbierski2013existence]].

## Contradictions and tensions
- [[sbierski2013existence]]: same gluing architecture; there the bad pairs are excluded by local uniqueness of the PDE, here they must be assumed absent. Complementary, not contradictory.
- None with [[eperon2018predictability]] or [[abbrescia2025quasilinear]].

## Open questions
- Does a maximal regular future g-boundary extension exist forcing absence of *all* intertwined timelike geodesics (Remark 47)?
- Can regularity be lowered to C^{1,1} or C^1 in the compatibility results (§6)?
- Combining with Sbierski's local uniqueness to get uniqueness of the metric on the boundary.
